## Overview

A non-profit residential building offering subsidised rent to artists, operated by a nonprofit called **ARTLIFE**. Home of missing artist [Abigail Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/). Originally built in 1921 as a private residence for Henry M. Lundine, converted to apartments in 1953 by architect [Asa Daribondi](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/asa-daribondi/). Its roof access door does not lead to a roof — it opens onto the [Night Floors](/locations/operation-alice/the-night-floors/).

**Destroyed by fire in Session 9** — M-Cell deliberately staged a gas explosion to contain the incursion, on Agent Marcus's tacit authorization.

## Known Apartments

- **1A** — Abigail Wright's apartment. The primary site of the FBI cataloguing operation across Sessions 1–5.
- **2B** — [Thomas Manuel's apartment](/locations/operation-alice/thomas-manuels-apartment/), across the hall from 1A.
- **3A** — [Roger Carun's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/roger-carun/) apartment, a cluttered mess with a home computer holding hidden files.
- **4A** — [Louis Post's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/louis-post/) apartment, a dump littered with pizza boxes and drawing supplies; scene of a violent knife attack in Session 7.
- **6B** — [Michelle Vanfitz's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/michelle-vanfitz/) apartment, whose interior opens onto an impossible library-lounge space far larger than the apartment itself.
- **Castaigne's apartment** — located within the Night Floors rather than the normal building; see [The Night Floors](/locations/operation-alice/the-night-floors/).
- **Basement** — contains Thomas Manuel's former painting room, old tenant storage rooms, and a boiler room. Post and Vanfitz were held here after being subdued in Session 7.

## Session Notes

### Session 1
- **Thursday night stakeout:** Mortimer and Marlowe staked out the building; broke in at 23:00. Found Wright's apartment in "ordered chaos" with a hidden microphone wired across the hallway.
- **Friday morning:** All five agents returned officially with Detective Giuradanda; recovered an FBI evidence box.

### Session 2
- Full-day catalogue of Wright's apartment (~25% complete by end of day). Marlowe heard voices through the wall. Mortimer interrogated Thomas Manuel across the hall.

### Session 3
- Team reconvened at 9:00 AM; found the police tape removed from Manuel's door. Mortimer discovered the Purson seal in Wright's apartment. Team split to interview tenants Roger Carun (3A), Michelle Vanfitz (6B, hostile), and Louis Post (4A).

### Session 4
- Ascended via the roof door into the Night Floors for the first time.

### Session 5
- Apartment 1A's catalogue was officially completed. The team checked Manuel's now-empty basement painting room — the linseed pails were gone.

### Session 6
- Returned at night; investigated Vanfitz's apartment (6B) after finding it ajar — a violent confrontation ended with Vanfitz shot and subdued.

### Session 7
- Confronted Louis Post (4A) in a violent knife attack. Both Post and Vanfitz were locked in the basement. Re-entered the Night Floors via a door in Vanfitz's library.

### Session 8
- The team exited the Night Floors for good after 12+ hours inside, in a state of collective breakdown.

### Session 9
- The team turned on the building's gas appliances and timed a spark to trigger an explosion, destroying the building entirely on Agent Marcus's tacit authorization.
