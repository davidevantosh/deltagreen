## Overview

A small, cluttered bookshop found by Mason and Murphy in Session 9, hidden behind a rusted security gate on an otherwise abandoned storefront — only faint gold paint scraps remain of a sign that once read "BOOKS." Narrow but very deep inside, with rows of leaning shelves lit by naked bulbs. Run by [Robert R. Robert ("Bob")](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/bob/).

This is the shop where [Abigail Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/) purchased her copy of *Le Roi en Jaune* on 22 February, per the [Farsi receipt](/evidence/operation-alice/farsi-receipt/) found in Session 2.

## Notable Finds

- A trapdoor "basement" deep in the stacks — barely large enough for one person, bare stone walls, dirt floor — where Bob fetched a second copy of the red book for Mortimer. Someone or something unseen handed him the book in the dark.
- A hidden clearing among the bookcases containing a [cache of period military gear](/evidence/operation-alice/gas-mask-cache/): a trench coat, gas mask, and loaded 1954-stamped shotgun, with two more identical crates stacked beneath.
- A locked strongbox containing [Bob's ledgers](/evidence/operation-alice/bobs-ledgers/), stolen by Marlowe and Mabel.
- [Moseby](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/moseby/), a man claiming to have authored *Le Roi en Jaune*, encountered wandering the stacks.
- The Hygromanteia, an old Latin occult tome, purchased by Mason.
