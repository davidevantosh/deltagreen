## Overview

Public park in NYC. Site of M-Cell's first meeting and briefing, arranged by [Agent Marcus](/npcs/delta-green/agent-marcus/).

## Session 1 Notes

- Marcus briefed the agents on [Operation ALICE](/cases/operation-alice/), then left behind his valise and departed.
- A [busking clown](/mysteries/the-clown/) performing here unsettled [Agent Mason](/characters/mason/), while [Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) noticed nothing unusual about the same act.
