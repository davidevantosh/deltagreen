## Overview

An abandoned farm east of [Groversville](/locations/operation-convergence/groversville/), located via cell tower triangulation of Murphy's missing hours.

## Session Notes

- **Session 13:** The barn housed a full Mi-Go biological operation — organic-mechanical instruments, operating tables, a vat of raw protomatter, inert humanoid husks, and two active Mi-Go performing surgery on a dead child. Destroyed by C4 planted by Mortimer during a firefight, though a rival faction extracted the surviving evidence and remains first.

## Open Threads

- The full scope of what the Mi-Go were producing here, and how long the operation had run, was never established.
