## Overview

A small rural Tennessee town, home to [Billy Ray Spivey](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/billy-ray-spivey/) and site of the events of Operation CONVERGENCE.

## Session Notes

- **Session 11:** The team investigates local cattle mutilation reports, UFO rumors, and Murphy's missing 12 hours.
- **Session 12:** Further missing-time incidents surface (Bud Aldrich, Jane Allen). Scott Adams is found dead in a motel bathroom.
- **Session 13:** Confirmed the town's water supply itself carries protomatter contamination. Ends with a mass-casualty event covered up as a hantavirus outbreak.
