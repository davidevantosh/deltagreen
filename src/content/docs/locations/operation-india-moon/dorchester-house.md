## Overview

A psychiatric facility in Boston, directed by [Dr. Richard Dallan](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/dr-dallan/), housing former Delta Green operatives with unnatural exposure histories.

## Session Notes

- **Session 16:** The team toured the facility, reviewing files on [the five missing operatives](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/the-five-missing-operatives/). Recognized [Morgan Goldstein](/npcs/bonds/morgan-goldstein/) and [David Langford](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/david-langford/) among the patients — both from Operation ALICE, twenty years prior. On the third floor ("the Pokey"), met [Ed Wist](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/ed-wist/) and [Tim Bael](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/tim-bael/), also recognized from the Night Floors. Security footage of the disappearances was found corrupted beyond recovery.

## Open Threads

- The five missing operatives were never found; the investigation was paused in favor of the Barbas threat and never resumed.
