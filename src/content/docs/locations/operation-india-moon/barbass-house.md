## Overview

Home of [Dr. Elias Barbas](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/elias-barbas/) ("Agent Exeter") in Medford, Massachusetts.

## Session Notes

- **Session 17 (first raid):** Team breached from front and back. Found the electricity off, an overwhelming smell of oil, smashed-but-clean dishes, and disassembled industrial machinery in the living room. A mechanical guardian, [the Lion](/mysteries/the-lion-and-the-scribe/), activated and critically wounded Mortimer. The team fled after setting the house on fire with gasoline; Barbas survived.
- **Session 18 (second raid):** The house was found undamaged, as if the first raid never happened. The Lion was destroyed with claymore mines and shotgun fire; Barbas was killed by a sniper shot. The team found [the Scribe](/evidence/operation-india-moon/the-scribe/), [the cheat sheets](/evidence/operation-india-moon/the-cheat-sheets/), [the Marbas seal](/evidence/operation-india-moon/the-marbas-seal/), and a barred closet door opening onto an impossible library. The house was burned a second time with a white phosphorus grenade, this time for good.
