## Overview

Apartment directly across the hall from [Abigail Wright's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/) in the [Macallister Building](/locations/macallister-building/), belonging to painter [Thomas Manuel](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/thomas-manuel/). The [hidden microphone](/evidence/hidden-microphone/) found in Session 1 was wired here.

## The Basement Painting Room

Manuel keeps a separate basement studio containing:

- A large blank canvas titled **"My Great Work."**
- Three pails of linseed oil, marked for pickup by "Sami/Esther."
- Three finished paintings — see [Manuel's Paintings](/mysteries/manuels-paintings/).

## Session 2 Notes

- [Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) interrogated Manuel here and confiscated his cassette tapes.
- After viewing the paintings, Mortimer ordered Manuel out and had him vacate to his parents' house.
- Once empty, Mortimer and [Mason](/characters/mason/) searched the apartment thoroughly — found it austere and hotel-like, with a fridge of meat and dairy still fresh despite "late March" expiry dates.
- Apartment sealed with crime tape after the search.
