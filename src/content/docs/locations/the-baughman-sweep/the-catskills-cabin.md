## Overview

A remote cabin owned by [Clyde Baughman](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/clyde-baughman/), found via paperwork in his apartment.

## Session Notes

- **Session 10:** Mason and Murphy found a Vietnam-era footlocker under the bed containing the [Manchester Shadow Team files](/evidence/the-baughman-sweep/manchester-shadow-team-files/), an [unopened Delta Green envelope](/evidence/the-baughman-sweep/the-unopened-envelope/), and other effects. A suspicious dig site near the property led to a padlocked septic tank hatch, from which a woman's voice pleaded for help — [That Which Was Marlene](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/that-which-was-marlene/), Baughman's transformed and imprisoned late wife. The confrontation ended with her burned alive.

## Open Threads
