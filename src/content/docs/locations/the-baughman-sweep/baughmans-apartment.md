## Overview

Home of deceased Delta Green field agent [Clyde Baughman](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/clyde-baughman/) in East New York.

## Session Notes

- **Session 10:** Swept by Mason and Murphy for Delta Green-related material. An unusual military-grade lock key was found on Baughman's keyring. Family photos throughout the apartment had his late wife Marlene's eyes scratched out of every picture. Little else of note was found beyond old paperwork — the real lead was a reference to his Catskills cabin.
