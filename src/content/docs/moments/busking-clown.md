Leaving the park, [Agent Mason](/characters/mason/) spots a child-sized clown busking to a looping, dirge-like record, dragging a ribbon-covered paper dragon through the air in precise, deliberate movements. The mask has a wide rectangular-toothed smile and black slit eyes. Onlookers barely react. Mason is deeply unsettled, tries to engage the crowd without success, leaves money on the stage, and hurries to catch up with the others.

[Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) sees the same performer and notices nothing unusual.

**Participants:** Agent Mason, Agent Mortimer
