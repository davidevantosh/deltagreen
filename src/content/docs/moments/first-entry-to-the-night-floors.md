[Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/), [Murphy](/characters/murphy/), and [Mason](/characters/mason/) ascend the Macallister Building to its roof access door. Mortimer opens it — and instead of the roof, bright light and old-timey music flood out. The door opens onto a turn-of-the-century men's club: velvet wallpaper, a roaring hearth, a wet bar, and a single occupant reading a book in an armchair. The [Night Floors](/locations/the-night-floors/) has been found.

**Participants:** Agent Mortimer, Agent Murphy, Agent Mason
