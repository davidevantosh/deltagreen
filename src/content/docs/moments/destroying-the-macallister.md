With Agent Marcus's tacit authorization and confirmation that no innocents remain inside, M-Cell returns to the Macallister Building at 3:00 AM. Marlowe distracts a nosy police officer while the others turn on every gas appliance in Wright's empty apartment; Mabel uses his electrical knowledge to time a spark. The team leaves. The building explodes into flames — "a sad accident" — and by the time fire crews arrive, there's nothing left worth saving.

**Participants:** All of M-Cell
