Deep in the Night Floors, the team finds a man in his thirties tied to a medical table, begging to be freed. He identifies himself as "Agent Vega," insists the year is 2015, and — when asked about Michael Witwer — screams that he IS Michael Witwer, a DEA agent. Before the team can untie him, the floor opens and the table retracts into the darkness despite the agents grabbing hold. Looking down into the hole, they see a distant, pale face with empty eye sockets staring back up at them.

**Participants:** Agent Mortimer, Agent Murphy, Agent Mason, "Agent Vega" / Michael Witwer
