Checking on a silent Louis Post, [Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) boots his door open to find the apartment dark and empty-seeming — until Post launches from the shadows with a carving knife. [Murphy](/characters/murphy/) narrowly dodges the first swing; [Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/) rushes to disarm him and is cut deeply across the arm and torso, bleeding heavily. Mortimer unloads three rounds into Post, who barely reacts; Murphy grabs the knife, and Marlowe finishes him with a flurry of kicks and punches, leaving him unconscious and bleeding out.

**Participants:** Agent Mortimer, Agent Murphy, Agent Marlowe, Louis Post
