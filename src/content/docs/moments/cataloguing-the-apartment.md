With latex gloves on, [Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/), [Mason](/characters/mason/), [Mabel](/characters/mabel/), and [Murphy](/characters/murphy/) spend the day cataloguing [Abigail Wright's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/) apartment while [Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) breaks off to investigate the neighbor across the hall. They surface several items of interest — a [Farsi receipt](/evidence/farsi-receipt/), [Hotel Broadalbin letterhead](/evidence/hotel-broadalbin-letterhead/), a [play transcript page](/evidence/play-transcript-page/), a [butcher paper map](/evidence/butcher-paper-map/), and the [impossible mechanical diagrams](/evidence/mechanical-diagrams/) — which Murphy keeps on his person rather than bagging with the rest. By day's end the team is roughly 25% through the FBI assignment.

**Participants:** Agent Marlowe, Agent Mason, Agent Mabel, Agent Murphy
