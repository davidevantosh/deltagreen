While [Mason](/characters/mason/) distracts [Castaigne](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/castaigne/) for a basement key, [Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/) picks the lock to his bedroom and finds a huge handwritten genealogical tome, *The Imperial Dynasty of America*. Castaigne notices her looking and flies into an immediate, murderous rage. [Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) restrains the old man and pushes him into an armchair; Castaigne pouts and mutters to himself in an unknown language.

**Participants:** Agent Mortimer, Agent Marlowe, Agent Mason, Castaigne
