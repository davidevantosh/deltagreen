[Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) gets back to White Plains and finds a white origami paper dragon on his front doorstep — matching the paper dragon dragged by the clown busker in [Washington Square Park](/locations/operation-alice/washington-square-park/) during Session 1. He screws it up and throws it over his shoulder before heading inside, destroying it without examination.
