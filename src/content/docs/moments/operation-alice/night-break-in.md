After a quiet stakeout turns up nothing, [Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) and [Agent Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/) let themselves into the [Macallister Building](/locations/operation-alice/macallister-building/) and make their way to [Abigail Wright's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/) apartment. Flashlights reveal a scene of "ordered chaos." Marlowe has a creeping sense of being watched. Mortimer finds a [hidden microphone](/evidence/operation-alice/hidden-microphone/) under the door and traces its wire to the apartment across the hall — but they decide not to pursue it as midnight strikes.

On the way out, they empty Wright's mailbox. Marlowe attempts to pick a neighboring mailbox, fails, and drops her [lockpick set](/evidence/operation-alice/lockpick-set/) with a loud noise, forcing an immediate exit.

**Participants:** Agent Mortimer, Agent Marlowe
