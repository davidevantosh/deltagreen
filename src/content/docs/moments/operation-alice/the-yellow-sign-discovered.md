While cataloguing with [Mason](/characters/mason/), [Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/) finds an occult symbol scrawled on a napkin hidden behind a sheet in Wright's apartment. As soon as it's exposed, a wave of melancholy, anxiety, and paranoia settles over the room. Mason looks up and sees nothing unusual — but Marlowe is hit hard: creeping horror, weak knees, and an immediate, unshakeable urge to share what she's found.

**Participants:** Agent Marlowe, Agent Mason
