After 12+ hours inside, the team finally steps back down into reality — and the weight of everything they've seen lands at once. [Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) empties his pistol into a wall, screaming, then sprints downstairs and collapses on the sidewalk, hyperventilating, his old tomahawk wound bleeding fresh. Behind him, spiders pour from the bullet-riddled wall. [Murphy](/characters/murphy/) and [Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/) break down sobbing and flee too, [David Langford](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/david-langford/) close behind. Mason notices the golden goldfish sculpture in his hands has become living flesh, flopping helplessly. The team eats in trauma-bonded silence afterward — M-Cell is their family now.

**Participants:** All of M-Cell, David Langford
