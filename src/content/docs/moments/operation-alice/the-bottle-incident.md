[Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/) (real name Miya) arrives at [Sal's deli](/npcs/bonds/sal-mazzi/) to help move boxes as promised. A large man in an old suit accosts her, screaming: "Where's my bottle? Marlowe, you fucking bitch. Where's my bottle? Where is it?" Sal scares him off with an old firearm. Marlowe finishes the job and receives a bag of cold cuts. Her neighbor [Peggy Funk](/npcs/bonds/peggy-funk/) tries to say hello on the way home; Marlowe coldly rebuffs her.

**Participants:** Agent Marlowe, Man in an Old Suit, Sal Mazzi, Peggy Funk
