[Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) crosses the hall to interrogate [Thomas Manuel](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/thomas-manuel/), the 26-year-old painter the [hidden microphone's](/evidence/operation-alice/hidden-microphone/) wire led to. Manuel claims to genuinely have no idea why it was there. Mortimer confiscates his [cassette tapes](/evidence/operation-alice/manuels-cassette-tapes/) and asks to see his basement painting area.

There, Mortimer finds [Manuel's Paintings](/mysteries/manuels-paintings/) — including one depicting the exact clown from [Washington Square Park](/locations/operation-alice/washington-square-park/) that unsettled Mason in Session 1. Disturbed, Mortimer cuffs Manuel and gives him a hard grilling, then orders him to vacate the apartment and stay with his parents. Manuel agrees.

**Participants:** Agent Mortimer, Thomas Manuel
