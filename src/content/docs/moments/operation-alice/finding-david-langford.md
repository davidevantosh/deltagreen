A door creaks open near the exhausted, shaken team, and a man steps out with his hands raised — emaciated, haunted, his beard grown out and hair filthy. He gives his name as David Langford, a cable repairman who entered the Macallister on 20 March 1995 and has been trapped in the Night Floors ever since, though he's lost track of exactly how long. He falls to his knees, begging the agents to get him out.
