While cataloguing Wright's apartment, [Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) finds a golden seal reading **PURSON** mounted on the wall. As he removes it to show the others, a disorienting blast of noise fills the room — resolving, as their senses recover, into a mundane taxi horn and a homeless man crossing the street with a large snake. Mason recognizes the seal as demonic, from the Ars Goetia.

**Participants:** Agent Mortimer, Agent Mason
