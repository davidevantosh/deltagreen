[Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) finds a small red book — *Le Roi en Jaune* — at Michelle Vanfitz's bedside and bags it despite her protests that "Abby" gave it to her. Vanfitz maces him, then attacks with a tomahawk, cutting his arm. In the struggle, Mortimer punches her, Marlowe pins her arms, and Mortimer shoots her in the arm to end it. Mabel dresses both wounds before the team cuffs her and locks her in her own bedroom.

**Participants:** Agent Mortimer, Agent Marlowe, Agent Mabel, Michelle Vanfitz
