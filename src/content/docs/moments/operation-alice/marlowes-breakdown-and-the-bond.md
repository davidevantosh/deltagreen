When Mortimer's discovery that an entire hallway is a plywood stage set sends a theater full of marionettes rising to disperse into the halls, it's the final straw for [Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/) — a full emotional breakdown, sobbing uncontrollably. [Mason](/characters/mason/) stops to comfort her, reassuring her they're professionals and they'll get through it. As she calms down, Mason shares his real name — Calvin Cooper — and Marlowe shares hers in return: Miya Fukazawa. The two form an explicit Delta Green Bond.

**Participants:** Agent Marlowe, Agent Mason
