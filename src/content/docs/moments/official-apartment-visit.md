M-Cell regroups outside the [Macallister Building](/locations/macallister-building/) and is met by [Detective Giuradanda](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/detective-giuradanda/), who shows them Wright's apartment, hands them his card, and departs. As they file in, [Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) discreetly crushes the [hidden microphone](/evidence/hidden-microphone/) underfoot. The team discovers the [FBI evidence box](/evidence/fbi-evidence-box/) left in the chaos.
