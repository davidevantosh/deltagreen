## Overview

One of five agents activated into M-Cell for Operation ALICE. **Real name: Miya Fukazawa.** Lives in **Chinatown**. Skilled lockpick. By Session 7, her own name turned up on a decades-old photograph found deep in the Night Floors — one of the campaign's most personally unsettling discoveries, still unresolved.

## Injuries & Ailments

- **Deep lacerations to the arm and torso** — inflicted by Louis Post's carving knife during the Session 7 struggle in his apartment; described as bleeding heavily. Treated by Mabel.
- **Fall injury** — hurt herself falling while trying to hop a balcony to chase a masked figure who'd left $10,000 in cash on her doorstep (Session 6).
- **Severe psychological trauma** — a full emotional breakdown (uncontrollable sobbing) after the stage-set reveal deep in the Night Floors (Session 7); broke down again exiting the Night Floors for good in Session 8.
- Noted personality shift toward coldness and hostility with her neighbor Peggy Funk across Sessions 2, 6, and 8 — from rebuffing a hello, to hiding from her, to slapping a plate of cookies out of her hands.

## Relationships

- **[Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/)** — stakeout/break-in partner in Session 1.
- **Sal Mazzi** — neighborhood deli owner.
- **Peggy Funk** — neighbor; the relationship has curdled significantly over the course of the case.
- **Miranda** — friend; the two have a standing manicure date.
- **Agent Mason** — formed an explicit Delta Green Bond with Mason in Session 8, after he comforted her through a breakdown and the two shared their real names.

## Home

Chinatown.

## Session Notes

### Session 1
- Partnered with Agent Mortimer to stake out the Macallister Building; broke into Wright's apartment with him overnight.
- Dropped her lockpick set loudly while attempting a neighboring mailbox, forcing an early exit.

### Session 2
- Heard voices through the wall while cataloguing Wright's apartment; investigated with Murphy, found nobody.
- Reviewed Wright's mail — utility termination notices from March, yet services still running.
- Accosted by an unidentified man outside Sal's deli, demanding "his bottle."

### Session 3
- Continued cataloguing the apartment with Mabel through the day.

### Session 4
- Investigated Roger Carun's apartment with Mabel, uncovering a rewritten play script and a hidden cryptic message.
- Present for the discovery of the two marionette "bodies" and Agent Vega's disappearance.

### Session 5
- Found an occult symbol — later identified as the Yellow Sign — scrawled on a napkin hidden behind a sheet in Wright's apartment. Hit hardest of anyone in the room: creeping horror, weak knees, and a lingering compulsion to "share" it.
- Missed her train to a manicure date with Miranda due to the lingering fear; the manicurist made an odd, echoing comment about her "steady hands." Took a white chess queen piece left mysteriously by her hand.
- Went to City Hall with Mabel, retrieving the building's blueprints, permits, and the original 1921 building permit.
- Gave in to the compulsion on the way home, tagging the side of a building with the Yellow Sign.

### Session 6
- Researched architect Asa Daribondi at the library with Mabel.
- Woken at 10:30 PM by a strange call and a knock; found $10,000 in cash left on her doorstep by a masked figure. Fell and injured herself trying to give chase over her balcony. Rebuffed Peggy Funk, who came to check on her.
- Present for the confrontation with Michelle Vanfitz, helping restrain her.

### Session 7
- Present for the discovery of the wall of photographs in the Night Floors — one captioned with her own real name, Miya Fukazawa, over an empty bottle.
- Attempted to disarm Louis Post and took the worst injuries of the fight — deep cuts to her arm and torso.
- Had a full emotional breakdown after the stage-set reveal; comforted by Mason, to whom she then revealed her real name after he shared his.

### Session 8
- Broke down again, sobbing, upon finally exiting the Night Floors after 12+ hours inside; fled the building.
- Slapped a plate of cookies out of Peggy Funk's hands the next day.

### Session 9
- Helped search for the bookshop with the rest of the team, and later helped steal the proprietor Bob's ledgers while Mortimer was distracted.
- Encountered Moseby, a man claiming to have written *Le Roi en Jaune*, deep in the bookshop's stacks.
- Present for the decision to destroy the Macallister Building, and for the final stand-down from Agent Marcus.
