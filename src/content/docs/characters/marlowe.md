## Overview

One of five agents activated into M-Cell for Operation ALICE. **Real name: Miya Fukazawa.** Originally in Chinatown. Skilled lockpick.

**As of 2015 (age 50):** Still in the same Chinatown apartment. Has not worked with Delta Green since 1995 until the events of Operation INDIA MOON. Runs a small, mostly cybercrime-focused criminal syndicate, with hacker "Acid" (4C1D) as her right hand; quietly wealthy but deliberately unassuming. Has a sleep disorder, is adapted to violence, and is a formidable unarmed combatant. Twenty years apart from the rest of M-Cell has done nothing to soften her.

## Injuries & Ailments

- **Deep lacerations to the arm and torso, Operation ALICE** — inflicted by Louis Post's carving knife.
- **Fall injury, Operation ALICE** — hurt herself chasing a masked figure over her balcony.
- **Severe psychological trauma, Operation ALICE** — full breakdowns both deep in the Night Floors and again upon finally exiting.
- **Sleep disorder** — ongoing as of 2015.
- **Adapted to violence** — a lasting personality shift by 2015, consistent with the coldness toward Peggy Funk first seen in 1995.

## Relationships

- **[Agent Mortimer / Dick Byrne](/characters/mortimer/)** — stakeout/break-in partner since 1995; carried him out of Barbas's house after "the Lion" nearly killed him.
- **Acid (4C1D)** — hacker, right-hand man in her criminal syndicate (as of 2015).
- **Agent Mason** — formed a Delta Green Bond with Mason during Operation ALICE.
- Real name and full Bond history exposed to the rest of M-Cell in Session 18, via Barbas's cheat sheets.

## Home

Chinatown, New York (1995 and 2015).

## Session Notes

### Session 1
- Partnered with Agent Mortimer to stake out the Macallister Building; broke into Wright's apartment with him overnight.
- Dropped her lockpick set loudly while attempting a neighboring mailbox, forcing an early exit.

### Session 2
- Heard voices through the wall while cataloguing Wright's apartment; investigated with Murphy, found nobody.
- Reviewed Wright's mail — utility termination notices from March, yet services still running.
- Accosted by an unidentified man outside Sal's deli, demanding "his bottle."

### Session 3
- Continued cataloguing the apartment with Mabel through the day.

### Session 4
- Investigated Roger Carun's apartment with Mabel, uncovering a rewritten play script and a hidden cryptic message.
- Present for the discovery of the two marionette "bodies" and Agent Vega's disappearance.

### Session 5
- Found an occult symbol — later identified as the Yellow Sign — scrawled on a napkin hidden behind a sheet in Wright's apartment. Hit hardest of anyone in the room.
- Missed her train to a manicure date with Miranda due to the lingering fear; the manicurist made an odd, echoing comment about her "steady hands." Took a white chess queen piece left mysteriously by her hand.
- Went to City Hall with Mabel, retrieving the building's blueprints and permits.
- Gave in to the compulsion on the way home, tagging the side of a building with the Yellow Sign.

### Session 6
- Researched architect Asa Daribondi at the library with Mabel.
- Woken at 10:30 PM by a strange call and a knock; found $10,000 in cash left on her doorstep by a masked figure. Fell and injured herself trying to give chase.
- Present for the confrontation with Michelle Vanfitz, helping restrain her.

### Session 7
- Present for the discovery of the wall of photographs in the Night Floors — one captioned with her own real name, Miya Fukazawa.
- Attempted to disarm Louis Post and took the worst injuries of the fight — deep cuts to her arm and torso.
- Had a full emotional breakdown after the stage-set reveal; comforted by Mason, to whom she then revealed her real name.

### Session 8
- Broke down again, sobbing, upon finally exiting the Night Floors after 12+ hours inside; fled the building.
- Slapped a plate of cookies out of Peggy Funk's hands the next day.

### Session 9
- Helped search for the bookshop with the rest of the team, and later helped steal the proprietor Bob's ledgers while Mortimer was distracted.
- Encountered Moseby, a man claiming to have written *Le Roi en Jaune*, deep in the bookshop's stacks.
- Present for the decision to destroy the Macallister Building, and for the final stand-down from Agent Marcus.

### Session 15
- Received the birthday invitation; deliberately avoided acknowledging Des Butler on the shared train to Boston, sitting elsewhere.
- Booked a room at the five-star Langham hotel, paying cash.
- Frosty with the rest of the reunited team, twenty years on.
- Present for Agent Exeter's Operation INDIA MOON briefing.

### Session 16
- Toured Dorchester House with the team.

### Session 17
- Present for the raid on Barbas's house — snuck in from the back with Mortimer.
- Recovered the Sitri-marked burner phone from Barbas's kitchen.
- Set the house on fire with gasoline to cover the team's escape.
- Carried Mortimer's dying body out of the house and drove him to the hospital, evading the pursuing Lion.

### Session 18
- Present for the second raid on Barbas's house — shot and destroyed the Lion's central gearbox.
- Drove the getaway vehicle after the house was set ablaze a second time.
- Read the cheat sheets with the rest of M-Cell — real names, Bonds, and secrets now known to everyone.
