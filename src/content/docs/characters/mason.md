## Overview

One of five agents activated into M-Cell for Operation ALICE. **Real name: Calvin Cooper.** Originally in Harlem with partner **Gloria**. Speaks French and Latin, with deep occult knowledge. His younger brother died young, drowned in the Hudson in an unsolved case.

**As of 2015 (age 57):** Two children, Carlos (18) and Aiyana (16); lives in Maplewood, New Jersey. His planned Catholic exposé was ultimately published by "real journalists," and Calvin pivoted into television writing, alongside continuing the Nightsea series he once ghost-wrote. When his literary agent Morgan Goldstein was "disappeared" by Delta Green after publishing one too many Yellow Sign-inflected works, Calvin took the warning and pulled back his own occult influences. Comfortably successful, but suffers from megalomania and obsession. Remains active with Delta Green — called on now for academic and occult expertise rather than fieldwork, though 2015 put him back in the middle of both.

## Injuries & Ailments

- **Directly read *Le Roi en Jaune* in full, Operation ALICE, Session 9** — "his mind is opened." Long-term effects still unclear as of 2015, but his subsequent decades of Castaigne obsession (see below) suggest something took hold.
- **Unknowingly propagated the Yellow Sign, Operation ALICE** — scrawled it into a manuscript sent to his agent.
- **Discovered an impossible photograph implicating Mortimer and his own partner Gloria, Operation ALICE** — never resolved, never disclosed to anyone but Marlowe.
- **Two decades of obsessive research into the Castaigne family**, following his 1995 purchase of the Hygromanteia — see [The Castaigne Dossier](/mysteries/the-castaigne-dossier/).
- **Torn-open arm, Operation INDIA MOON** — narrowly avoided decapitation by "the Lion"; badly injured instead.
- **Twisted ankle, Operation INDIA MOON** — fell fleeing the Lion during the first raid on Barbas's house.
- **Severe protomatter-related illness, Operation CONVERGENCE** — took months to recover.
- **Megalomania and obsession** — noted as ongoing personality traits by 2015.

## Session Notes

### Session 1
- Noticed and was unsettled by a busking clown in Washington Square Park.
- Partnered with Agent Mabel to follow up on Anthony Scott.
- Went home to Harlem; arranged a dinner date with Gloria.

### Session 2
- Helped catalogue Wright's apartment.
- Met Gloria for dinner; she said something strange he wasn't sure he actually heard.

### Session 3
- Called the Manuel family and fobbed off Thomas Manuel's desperate mother, Elaine.
- Went through Thomas Manuel's mail with the team, uncovering a menacing detail about the March visit from his parents.
- Recognized the golden Purson seal Mortimer found as a demonic sigil from the Ars Goetia.
- Interviewed Roger Carun, learning the building is run by a nonprofit called ARTLIFE.
- Found a photograph while cataloguing.

### Session 4
- Ascended into the Night Floors with Mortimer and Murphy; present for the discovery of the marionettes and Agent Vega's disappearance.

### Session 5
- Discovered an impossible photograph in a pornographic book depicting Mortimer with his own partner, Gloria; destroyed it without telling anyone (Marlowe saw it too).
- Was unaffected when Marlowe first showed the team the Yellow Sign, unlike Mortimer and Murphy.
- Visited his literary agent Morgan Goldstein, who pitched him on ghost-writing a new novel in a missing author's series.
- Researched the demon Purson online that evening.

### Session 6
- Present for the confrontation with Michelle Vanfitz; read the recovered red book aloud, identifying it as *Le Roi en Jaune* — *The King in Yellow.*

### Session 7
- Helped guard Vanfitz while the others checked on Louis Post.
- Recognized the invitation found on a clockwork figure in the Night Floors, addressed to a mysterious prisoner.
- Present for the discovery of the newspaper scrap bearing the Yellow Sign, and for the stage-set reveal that triggered Marlowe's breakdown — comforted her afterward, and the two exchanged real names.

### Session 8
- Led much of the search for an exit from the Night Floors.
- Found and recognized a ritual circle used to summon the demon Purson.
- Found a golden goldfish sculpture that later turned to living flesh in his hands.
- That night, dreamed of his brother's funeral and saw Asa Daribondi among the mourners; couldn't sleep afterward and began drinking.
- Scrawled the Yellow Sign into his current manuscript and sent it to his agent, reporting he "felt better."

### Session 9
- Helped search for the bookshop where Abigail bought her red book; found the Hygromanteia grimoire and a cache of gas masks and shotguns hidden among the stacks.
- Directly read *Le Roi en Jaune* — "his mind is opened."
- Present for the decision to destroy the Macallister Building and the final stand-down from Agent Marcus.

### Session 10
- Activated with Murphy for a routine Delta Green cleanup: sweeping the apartment of deceased field agent Clyde Baughman.
- Searched Baughman's apartment and Catskills cabin, finding the Vietnam-era footlocker.
- Confronted the entity in the septic tank ("That Which Was Marlene") with a manic episode of megalomania, nearly getting himself killed before the fight turned.
- Landed the killing blow with a chain and a match, ending Marlene's suffering.
- Exchanged real names with Murphy for the first time: "Calvin."

### Session 11
- Summoned alongside Murphy and Mortimer to assist FBI friendly James Derringer with the Billy Ray Spivey case.
- Present for Dr. Bennett's revelation about Spivey's non-human muscle tissue.

### Session 12
- Present for the interrogation of Billy Ray's friends and the confrontation with Bud Aldrich.
- Present for the discovery of Jane Allen and Scott Adams's body.

### Session 13
- Extracted Jane Allen to Dr. Bennett's care alongside her.
- Present for the raid on the Mi-Go farm.
- Suffered severe illness from protomatter exposure alongside Mabel; took months to recover.

### Session 14
- Moved into television writing after his Catholic exposé was published by others; injected King in Yellow influence into his own show.
- His agent Morgan Goldstein was "disappeared" by Delta Green after one too many Yellow Sign-inflected publications — Calvin took the warning and reined himself in.
- Spent years obsessively researching the Castaigne family after reading their name in the Hygromanteia; see [The Castaigne Dossier](/mysteries/the-castaigne-dossier/).

### Session 15
- Received the birthday invitation; flew up to Boston separately, staying at the Holiday Inn near Dorchester.
- Present for Agent Exeter's Operation INDIA MOON briefing; asked Exeter directly why the team was chosen.
- Researched Dr. Dallan online, finding nothing unusual.

### Session 16
- Found a live goldfish in his hotel sink upon waking.
- Recognized Morgan Goldstein and David Langford among the Dorchester House patients — both pretended not to know him.

### Session 17
- Present for the raid on Barbas's house — breached the front door.
- Narrowly avoided decapitation by "the Lion," suffering a torn-open arm instead; twisted his ankle fleeing.

### Session 18
- Saw Esther Samigina watching him through his own bathroom mirror from what appeared to be a different location entirely.
- Researched Ophelia Sitri and Michael Witwer, and recognized the Samigina name from the Macallister Building's basement notes, twenty years prior.
- Present for the second raid on Barbas's house; found the Scribe, the red notebook shipment, and the cheat sheets exposing everyone's real identities.
- Glimpsed an impossible library through a barred door in Barbas's closet.
