## Overview

One of five agents activated into M-Cell for Operation ALICE. **Real name: Dick Byrne.** Originally from White Plains. Ex-wife **Sarah Clarke** is a DEA agent. Work partner (1995) was **Reggie**.

**As of 2015 (age 62):** U.S. Marshal for the Southern District of New York from 2001–2015 — appointed by President George W. Bush. Snubbed Reggie for Chief Deputy in favor of Henry "Bass" Reeves, ending the friendship. Now divorced, isolated, living alone in a White Plains McMansion, with a Catskills cabin as a retreat. Diagnosed (informally, by pattern of behavior) with intermittent explosive disorder and a totemic compulsion regarding his 9mm pistol.

Of the five agents, Mortimer took the heaviest and most direct exposure to the Night Floors in 1995, and has continued to be the one most often on the front line of violence in every operation since — a pattern that nearly killed him in 2015.

## Injuries & Ailments

- **Critical injury, Operation INDIA MOON** — crushed against a kitchen wall by "the Lion," a mechanical entity, in Session 17: broken ribs, ruptured internal organs. Stabilized in emergency surgery, discharged himself against medical advice the following morning to finish the mission.
- **Tomahawk laceration, right arm (1995)** — inflicted by Michelle Vanfitz. Reopened during his breakdown exiting the Night Floors in Session 8.
- **Severe psychological trauma, Operation ALICE** — full breakdown exiting the Night Floors: screaming, emptying his sidearm into a wall, fleeing in a state of panic.
- **Dissociative episode, Operation ALICE** — answered a live transmission as "Alan," a name with no known connection to him.
- **Recurring nightmares** — his father's locked WWII diary and a childhood family argument (1995); briefly, mentally returned to the Night Floors during Reggie's deposition hearing.
- **Intermittent explosive disorder** and a **totemic attachment to his sidearm** — both noted as ongoing by 2015.

## Session Notes

### Session 1
- Partnered with Agent Marlowe to stake out the [Macallister Building](/locations/operation-alice/macallister-building/).
- Broke into Abigail Wright's apartment with Marlowe after the stakeout turned up nothing by 23:00.
- Found a hidden microphone under the apartment door; traced the wire to the apartment across the hall but chose not to pursue it further.
- Returned Friday morning and crushed the hidden microphone underfoot in front of Detective Giuradanda.

### Session 2
- Excused himself early from the group apartment search to investigate [Thomas Manuel](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/thomas-manuel/), the neighbor across the hall the hidden microphone's wire led to.
- Interrogated Manuel, confiscated his cassette tapes, and had him show his basement painting room — found [Manuel's Paintings](/mysteries/manuels-paintings/), one of which depicted the clown scene from Session 1.
- Cuffed and grilled Manuel, then ordered him to vacate and stay with his parents.
- Received an urgent page from Sarah Clarke — met her the next day at Central Park.
- Found a white origami paper dragon on his own doorstep in White Plains.

### Session 3
- Waited for the team outside the Macallister at 9:00 AM. Unlocked Thomas Manuel's mailbox and handed his mail to Mason.
- While cataloguing Wright's apartment, found a golden seal reading **PURSON** mounted on the wall. Removing it triggered a brief, disorienting blast of noise that resolved into mundane street sounds.
- Heard scratching at the apartment door; investigated and found a slightly chewed, slime-covered red rubber ball in the empty hallway.
- Met his ex-wife Sarah at a Central Park coffee shop — a frosty meeting; she left him paperwork due by 8:30 PM Sunday and dropped a lit cigarette in his coffee.
- Picked up audio surveillance gear from the US Marshals office. Returned and was confronted by Thomas Manuel himself in his own apartment. Backed off, but returned Manuel's keys with a hidden audio bug installed.
- That night, discovered a military-surplus walkie-talkie backpack unit fused to the apartment wall by resin. Powering it on played back a recording of his own voice interrogating Manuel, and Mason's voice warning about "those things" killing him.
- Saw an unknown man in white shoes, carrying a briefcase, pass rapidly across the open bathroom doorway — the bathroom was empty and had no other exit.

### Session 4
- Answered a live transmission on the recovered walkie-talkie, introducing himself as "Alan."
- Led the team up to the Macallister's roof access door, which opened instead onto the **[Night Floors](/locations/operation-alice/the-night-floors/)** — a turn-of-the-century men's club.
- Met Mark Roark, who directed the team toward the night manager, Castaigne.
- Distracted Castaigne with a locket recovered from Manuel's apartment so Marlowe could search his room; when Castaigne caught her, he flew into a rage, and Mortimer restrained him.
- Found two "dead" marionettes down a hallway carrying period ID cards for Eric K. Carter and Ronald Burbach.
- Was present when the man calling himself "Agent Vega" (Michael Witwer) was swallowed into the floor on a retracting medical table.

### Session 5
- Grabbed a bottle of brandy from the smoking lounge's wet bar and left immediately — did not see the pornographic book that later shocked Mason.
- The team escaped the Night Floors to find barely a minute had passed outside. Handed the Wright apartment keys to Marlowe before heading home.
- Sunday: did paperwork in the morning, then drove to the apartment.
- Was visibly shaken (along with Murphy) when Marlowe showed the team the occult sign — Yellow Sign — she'd found; described as "almost at his breaking point."
- That evening, Sarah returned to collect her paperwork, apologized for her earlier behavior, and gave him a 9mm suppressor.

### Session 6
- Attended a deposition hearing in court for his partner Reggie; briefly, disturbingly found himself mentally back in the Night Floors before snapping back when the judge called his name.
- Went for beers with Reggie afterward.
- Later that night, was present at the Vanfitz apartment when Michelle Vanfitz attacked the group with mace and a tomahawk after he bagged her copy of *Le Roi en Jaune* — took a tomahawk cut to the arm before shooting her in the arm to end the struggle.

### Session 7
- Watched the basement door as Post and Vanfitz were locked away, leaving the Delta Green handoff box outside.
- Led the second Night Floors excursion of the night. Calmed the mastiff Abe by retrieving its ball.
- Found the long crack in the plaster that revealed the entire hallway to be a stage set in front of an audience of marionettes.
- Present for the discovery of David Langford, a man trapped in the Night Floors since March.

### Session 8
- Present for the naming of the Delta Green Bond between Mason and Marlowe.
- Took point navigating by scent with his eyes shut when the group grew desperate for an exit; found no exit this way.
- Upon finally exiting the Night Floors after 12+ hours inside, suffered a full breakdown — screaming, emptying his pistol into a wall, and fleeing the building; his tomahawk wound reopened.
- Went back into the building alone afterward to retrieve his discarded jacket and dropped gun.
- Called handler Agent Marcus to debrief; received the order to leave Post and Vanfitz's fate to Delta Green cleanup, and to take David Langford home overnight.
- Took David Langford home to White Plains for the night; two Delta Green agents collected Langford from his home the next morning.
- That night, dreamed of his father's locked WWII diary and a family argument from childhood.

### Session 9
- Called handler Agent Marcus alongside the team; confirmed the Macallister Building was top priority for urgent containment.
- Present for the decision to destroy the building — the team turned on the gas and left before it exploded.
- In the final debrief, Agent Marcus stood the team down to rest and lie low.
- Spotted the masked, white-shoed figure one last time near the alley outside, disappearing before the team could catch it — leaving behind a gramophone that began to play on its own.

### Session 11
- Received the anonymous summons alongside Mason and Murphy, but doesn't physically join the team on the ground in Groversville until Session 12.

### Session 12
- Arrived in Groversville and rejoined the team.
- Investigated the reservoir with Mabel; spotted a black helicopter circling overhead.
- Noticed and lost two separate tails — a white van (Frank Carincola) and a black sedan (an armed, government-affiliated stranger) — while driving around town.
- Present for the discovery of Jane Allen and Scott Adams's body.

### Session 13
- Scouted the Mi-Go breeding barn alone, discovering the full scope of the operation.
- Planted and detonated C4 on the barn, then fought off a Mi-Go patrol alongside Mabel and Murphy.
- Present for the arrival of the rival extraction team and their taunts about "Majestic-12."
- Drove Murphy at high speed to the nearest trauma center after his protomatter incisions catastrophically reopened.

### Session 15
- Surveilled Gateway Bridges Restaurant ahead of the Session 15 briefing and noted the arrival of a Massachusetts State Police vehicle — later identified as belonging to Dr. Elias Barbas.
- Had Barbas's plates traced through his own contacts, uncovering his real identity before the rest of the team.

### Session 16
- Toured Dorchester House with the team; recognized old faces from the Night Floors among the patients.
- Tailed orderly Richard Brice to a gym locker containing a mummified child's body; lost his composure and destroyed the evidence in a rage before fleeing the scene.
- Received Barbas's personnel file from Chief Deputy Bass Reeves.
- Staked out Barbas's house that night and found the cryptic postcard mentioning Abigail and Moseby.

### Session 17
- Breached Barbas's house from the back with Marlowe.
- Was cornered and nearly killed by "the Lion" — crushed against the kitchen wall, broken ribs, ruptured organs.
- Carried out of the house unconscious by Marlowe and rushed to the hospital.

### Session 18
- Discharged himself from the hospital against medical advice to rejoin the team.
- Sniped Barbas dead through an upstairs window during the second raid.
- Helped search the house afterward, recovering Barbas's personal effects including the key to "room 616."
- Torched the house with a white phosphorus grenade as the team fled.
- Read the cheat sheets with the rest of M-Cell — real names, Bonds, and secrets now known to everyone.
