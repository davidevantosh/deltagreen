## Overview

One of five agents activated into M-Cell for Operation ALICE. **Real name: Dutch Gaper.** Lives on **Jamaica Ave**. Divorced from **Jennifer**; they co-own a dog, Gonzo. By campaign's end, a hidden family mystery involving a safety deposit box and a relative named **Ari Gaper** has surfaced — unresolved and clearly personal.

## Injuries & Ailments

- **Severe psychological trauma** — following the Session 8 escape from the Night Floors after 12+ hours inside, suffered a breakdown alongside Marlowe: sobbing, fleeing the building.
- Resisted an intrusive compulsion to stare deeper into a mirror found in Louis Post's apartment (Session 7) — successfully self-corrected, laid the mirror face-down.
- No physical injuries recorded.

## Relationships

- **Jennifer** — ex-wife; they co-own Gonzo the dog. She still calls him for favors (closing a joint bank account).
- **[Ari Gaper](/mysteries/the-ari-gaper-box/)** — unknown relative; a safety deposit box registered in this name on Murphy's own family line surfaced as a major loose thread at campaign's end.
- **James** — old friend; dropped by unannounced in Session 5.
- **Sandra** — FBI office colleague who helped Murphy dig up records in Session 6.

## Home

Jamaica Ave. Owns a dog, Gonzo, currently living with ex-wife Jennifer.

## Session Notes

### Session 1
- Volunteered to take the FBI and police reports home for analysis rather than joining the field teams.
- Took an answerphone message from his ex-wife before diving into the paperwork.

### Session 2
- Joined the apartment search team cataloguing items of interest in Wright's apartment.
- Investigated strange voices with Marlowe — found nobody there.
- Personally held onto the day's items of interest rather than bagging them with the rest.

### Session 3
- Woken by his dog, Gonzo, for an early walk; reflected on his fellow agents before heading to the Macallister.
- Discovered the mechanical diagrams ("The Lion" and "The Scribe") napkins had gone missing from the evidence he was holding.
- Brought coffee for the team at the 9:00 AM meetup.
- Present for the discovery of the golden Purson seal and the walkie-talkie recording of Mortimer and Mason's voices.

### Session 4
- Ascended into the [Night Floors](/locations/the-night-floors/) with Mortimer and Mason. Present for the discovery of the two marionette "bodies" carrying Carter and Burbach's identification, and for Agent Vega's disappearance into the floor.

### Session 5
- Drove out to New Jersey to drop Gonzo off at Jennifer's old house; cried in the car afterward.
- Gave handler Agent Marcus a high-level briefing on the case's progress over the phone; received Thomas Wright's (Abigail's father's) contact details.
- His old friend James dropped by that evening for a visit.

### Session 6
- Researched Eric K. Carter and Ronald Burbach's military service records with Mortimer at the FBI office.
- Went to Chase Manhattan Bank to close a joint account with Jennifer — discovered a safety deposit box (616) registered in his name that he has no memory of opening. Paid a $50 fee to request records on it and a replacement key.
- Present for the confrontation with Michelle Vanfitz over the red book.

### Session 7
- Broke down the door to Louis Post's apartment and was ambushed by Post with a carving knife; dodged the first swing.
- Examined Post's disturbing drawings and resisted the pull of his mirror, taking one drawing as evidence.
- Helped march Post and Vanfitz down to the basement.
- Present for the discovery of the wall of "bottled" photographs, including one captioned with Marlowe's real name.

### Session 8
- Present for the Mason/Marlowe Bond moment.
- Followed his instincts through the Night Floors, spotting an aged version of a Chase Manhattan bank officer he recognized, and glimpsing a masked, gold-and-silver-clad figure through a projection room window moments before it was obscured.
- Broke down sobbing alongside Marlowe upon finally exiting the Night Floors; fled the building.

### Session 9
- Called Thomas Wright to arrange a meeting near the bookshop where Abigail bought her red book.
- Interrogated the bookshop's proprietor, Bob, alongside Mortimer about Abigail and the book's origins.
- Asked after *A World Without Doors* and its author, Emeline F. Fitzroy.
- Present for the decision to destroy the Macallister Building and the final stand-down from Agent Marcus.
- **Epilogue:** Went to Chase Manhattan Bank weeks later and finally opened his safety deposit box — registered in 1951 in the name of Ari Gaper, on that person's day of birth. Found a 19th-century jeweled crown inside. Photographed it, then locked it away again.
