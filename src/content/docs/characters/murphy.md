## Overview

One of five agents activated into M-Cell for Operation ALICE. **Real name: Dutch Gaper.** Originally on Jamaica Ave. Divorced from Jennifer since before 1995; a hidden family mystery involving a safety deposit box and a relative named **Ari Gaper** surfaced at the end of Operation ALICE.

**As of 2015 (age 64):** Still on Jamaica Ave, with a new dog, Fozzy, after ex-wife Jennifer moved to Michigan with her new husband and took Gonzo. Coasting toward retirement in a comfortable FBI desk job arranged by his now-estranged colleague Sandra. Writes weird fiction (*The Adventures of Drake Obsidian*) in his spare time and sees a therapist, Dr. Carolyn Crowell, to process his 2000 alien abduction. Semi-fluent in Tartessian, an extinct Bronze Age language he can't fully explain learning.

## Injuries & Ailments

- **Alien abduction and unexplained months in Delta Green custody, 2000–2001** — after nearly dying from protomatter-related organ failure, vanished from his hospital bed and has only fragmentary memory of the following months: bright lights, repeated surgeries, being drugged, and overhearing Delta Green discuss "putting him down, just to be safe." Woke on the pavement outside his own apartment in the winter of 2001 in a hospital gown. Never called up by Delta Green again for over a decade afterward.
- **Extensive protomatter surgical scarring, torso** — invisible scars from groin to neck, revealed under Dr. Bennett's detection compound (Session 13). Catastrophically reopened when the protomatter in his body went inert after the rival faction's extraction, nearly killing him.
- **Missing 12 hours, Groversville (Session 11)** — no memory of the time; returned dehydrated, later producing a fluorescent, oil-slick urine sample.
- **Weight gain and metabolic anomalies, Groversville** — gained approximately 5kg in ~8 hours with no bowel movements, constant thirst.
- **Adapted to helplessness** — a lasting psychological effect of the 2000–2001 abduction, per his own self-assessment by 2015.

## Relationships

- **Jennifer** — ex-wife; remarried and moved to Michigan with the dog, Gonzo.
- **Sandra** — FBI colleague; arranged his desk job, relationship soured since.
- **Dr. Carolyn Crowell** — therapist, treating him for his 2000 abduction trauma.
- **[Ari Gaper](/mysteries/the-ari-gaper-box/)** — unknown relative; the safety deposit box mystery from the end of Operation ALICE remains unresolved.
- Real name and full Bond history exposed to the rest of M-Cell in Session 18, via Barbas's cheat sheets.

## Home

Jamaica Ave, Queens. Owns a dog, Fozzy (as of 2015).

## Session Notes

### Session 1
- Volunteered to take the FBI and police reports home for analysis rather than joining the field teams.
- Took an answerphone message from his ex-wife before diving into the paperwork.

### Session 2
- Joined the apartment search team cataloguing items of interest in Wright's apartment.
- Investigated strange voices with Marlowe — found nobody there.
- Personally held onto the day's items of interest rather than bagging them with the rest.

### Session 3
- Woken by his dog, Gonzo, for an early walk; reflected on his fellow agents before heading to the Macallister.
- Discovered the mechanical diagrams ("The Lion" and "The Scribe") napkins had gone missing from the evidence he was holding.
- Brought coffee for the team at the 9:00 AM meetup.
- Present for the discovery of the golden Purson seal and the walkie-talkie recording of Mortimer and Mason's voices.

### Session 4
- Ascended into the [Night Floors](/locations/operation-alice/the-night-floors/) with Mortimer and Mason. Present for the discovery of the two marionette "bodies" carrying Carter and Burbach's identification, and for Agent Vega's disappearance into the floor.

### Session 5
- Drove out to New Jersey to drop Gonzo off at Jennifer's old house; cried in the car afterward.
- Gave handler Agent Marcus a high-level briefing on the case's progress over the phone; received Thomas Wright's (Abigail's father's) contact details.
- His old friend James dropped by that evening for a visit.

### Session 6
- Researched Eric K. Carter and Ronald Burbach's military service records with Mortimer at the FBI office.
- Went to Chase Manhattan Bank to close a joint account with Jennifer — discovered a safety deposit box (616) registered in his name that he has no memory of opening. Paid a $50 fee to request records on it and a replacement key.
- Present for the confrontation with Michelle Vanfitz over the red book.

### Session 7
- Broke down the door to Louis Post's apartment and was ambushed by Post with a carving knife; dodged the first swing.
- Examined Post's disturbing drawings and resisted the pull of his mirror, taking one drawing as evidence.
- Helped march Post and Vanfitz down to the basement.
- Present for the discovery of the wall of "bottled" photographs, including one captioned with Marlowe's real name.

### Session 8
- Present for the Mason/Marlowe Bond moment.
- Followed his instincts through the Night Floors, spotting an aged version of a Chase Manhattan bank officer he recognized, and glimpsing a masked, gold-and-silver-clad figure through a projection room window moments before it was obscured.
- Broke down sobbing alongside Marlowe upon finally exiting the Night Floors; fled the building.

### Session 9
- Called Thomas Wright to arrange a meeting near the bookshop where Abigail bought her red book.
- Interrogated the bookshop's proprietor, Bob, alongside Mortimer about Abigail and the book's origins.
- Asked after *A World Without Doors* and its author, Emeline F. Fitzroy.
- Present for the decision to destroy the Macallister Building and the final stand-down from Agent Marcus.
- **Epilogue:** Went to Chase Manhattan Bank weeks later and finally opened his safety deposit box — registered in 1951 in the name of Ari Gaper, on that person's day of birth. Found a 19th-century jeweled crown inside. Photographed it, then locked it away again.

### Session 10
- Activated with Mason for a routine Delta Green cleanup: sweeping the apartment of deceased field agent Clyde Baughman.
- Searched the apartment and, with Mason, drove to Baughman's Catskills cabin, finding a Vietnam-era footlocker.
- Discovered the pile of disturbed earth at the property's edge and the padlocked septic tank.
- Ignored the pleading voice from inside the tank at first; later helped subdue and destroy the entity within — "That Which Was Marlene."
- Exchanged real names with Mason for the first time at the end of the session: "Dutch."

### Session 11
- Summoned alongside Mason and Mortimer to assist FBI friendly James Derringer with the Billy Ray Spivey case.
- Present for Dr. Bennett's revelation about Spivey's non-human muscle tissue.
- Vanished mid-conversation at RR Diner while taking a call from his ex-wife; did not return that night.

### Session 12
- Reappeared the next morning in the motel bathroom with no memory of the missing 12 hours, badly dehydrated.
- Provided a urine sample that came back a fluorescent, oil-slick color.
- Present for the discovery of Jane Allen and Scott Adams's body.

### Session 13
- Revealed to have extensive invisible protomatter scarring across his torso under Dr. Bennett's detection compound.
- Present for the raid on the Mi-Go breeding farm.
- Suffered catastrophic organ failure when his protomatter scarring reopened after the rival faction neutralized the site's protomatter; rushed to a trauma center by Mortimer, surviving emergency surgery.
- Vanished from the hospital shortly after into unexplained Delta Green custody, not returning to himself for months.

### Session 14
- Never called up by Delta Green again for over a decade after his 2000–2001 ordeal.
- Spent the interim years processing the abduction in therapy, writing weird fiction, and inexplicably becoming semi-fluent in Tartessian.

### Session 15
- Received the birthday invitation summons; drove up to Boston with Mortimer.
- Present for Agent Exeter's Operation INDIA MOON briefing.

### Session 16
- Toured Dorchester House with the team.
- Present for the discovery of the corrupted security footage and the mummified child's body.

### Session 17
- Present for the raid on Barbas's house and the first encounter with "the Lion."
- Recovered the Sitri-marked burner phone from Barbas's kitchen.
- Helped get Mortimer to the hospital after his near-fatal injury.

### Session 18
- Present for the second raid on Barbas's house.
- Helped clear the search of the upstairs bedrooms, uncovering the Scribe and the red notebook shipment.
- Read the cheat sheets with the rest of M-Cell — real names, Bonds, and secrets now known to everyone.
