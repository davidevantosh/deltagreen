## Overview

One of five agents activated into M-Cell for Operation ALICE. **Real name: Des Butler.** Lives in **Brooklyn** with his wife, **Teri**. Has a day job with coworker **Barry** and boss **Barrett**. Knows Latin and has deep knowledge of the occult — the most academically prepared member of M-Cell for what they found in the Night Floors, and consequently one of the hardest hit by it.

## Injuries & Ailments

- **Severe compulsion/fugue episodes tied to the Yellow Sign** — after seeing the sign in Session 5, developed a recurring urge to "share" it (drawing it unconsciously, feeling compelled to show others). By Session 8, entered a full catatonic fugue state — unable to get out of bed for most of a day, unable to sleep until he shared the sign, but unable to make himself move.
- **Lost a full day** — woke Wednesday morning (Session 9) with no memory of Tuesday, having spent it in a fever dream about the Night Floors. Missed a scheduled team meetup as a result.
- **Recurring nightmares** of the Night Floors, ongoing as of Session 9.

## Relationships

- **Teri** — wife. Left him a tape recording, cut off mid-sentence, ending with her shouting "HAVE YOU SEEN IT?" after his lost day.
- **Abad** — poker buddy, knows Mabel by his real first name, "Des."
- **Barry** and **Barrett** — coworker and civilian boss at his day job.

## Home

Brooklyn.

## Session Notes

### Session 1
- Recognized by poker buddy Abad at the pizza restaurant — quickly ushered him out to protect the team's cover.
- Partnered with Agent Mason to follow up on Anthony Scott.
- Went home to Brooklyn; wife was on night shift.

### Session 2
- Spent the day cataloguing items of interest in Wright's apartment.
- Came home to a note from his wife about spending Sunday together.

### Session 3
- Left the group around 5:30 PM for a poker game on Long Island with Abad. A fellow player, thinking Mabel was in AA, asked if he'd heard of some "encounter group" — he hadn't.

### Session 4
- Checked in on the team from the street after his poker game, tapping on the apartment window.
- Investigated Roger Carun's apartment with Marlowe — found a rewritten copy of "the play" and a set of cryptic short stories spelling out a hidden phrase.

### Session 5
- Had lunch with his wife Teri; felt watched throughout but couldn't identify why.
- Was present when Marlowe showed the team the Yellow Sign — unaffected himself, unlike Mortimer and Murphy.
- Got called into work by coworker Barry at his boss Barrett's insistence.
- Went with Marlowe to City Hall, where they obtained the building's blueprints and permits, including the original 1921 building permit.

### Session 6
- Helped research architect Asa Daribondi at the library with Marlowe, uncovering his suspected history of child murders and his connection to the Hotel Broadalbin.
- Present for the confrontation with Michelle Vanfitz; dressed both her gunshot wound and Mortimer's tomahawk cut.

### Session 7
- Stayed behind to guard the restrained Vanfitz while Mortimer, Murphy, and Marlowe checked on Louis Post.
- Found a scrap of a 1905 newspaper bearing the Yellow Sign in the Night Floors — showing it to the team triggered the shared, involuntary "have you seen the Yellow Sign?" moment.
- Present for the discovery of David Langford.

### Session 8
- Present for the naming of the Mason/Marlowe Bond.
- Helped guide the search for an exit from the Night Floors.
- Suffered a severe fugue episode overnight and into Tuesday — unable to get out of bed, compelled but unable to share the Yellow Sign.

### Session 9
- Lost all of Tuesday to the fugue state; woke Wednesday with a disturbing tape message from his wife.
- Read two recovered occult texts, *A World Without Doors* and *A History of the Russo-Germanic Hegemony*.
- Gathered the team at Madison Square Park via burner beeper once he recovered; the search for the Hotel Broadalbin turned up nothing.
- Read the stolen bookshop ledgers — found only "Jabberwocky" translated into Farsi, repeated for a thousand pages, dated back to 1895.
- Present for the decision to destroy the Macallister Building — personally used his electrical knowledge to time the gas explosion.
