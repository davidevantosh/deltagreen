## What Happened

During Operation ALICE (Session 2), the team found two napkins in Abigail Wright's apartment bearing impossible mechanical diagrams, labeled **"The Lion"** and **"The Scribe."** The napkins later went missing from evidence.

Twenty years later, during Operation INDIA MOON, both devices turned out to be real:

- **The Lion** — a mechanical guardian entity at Elias Barbas's house, which nearly killed Mortimer in Session 17 before being destroyed with claymore mines and shotgun fire in Session 18.
- **The Scribe** — an automated writing/binding machine found in Barbas's house in Session 18, alongside a supply of blank red leather notebooks matching the stock of the original *Le Roi en Jaune.*

## Why It's Flagged

These were dismissed as "impossible" sketches in 1995 — clearly, they weren't. Someone built both devices in the intervening twenty years, or they existed all along somewhere M-Cell never found.

## Current Theories

- Barbas (or whoever/whatever he served) may have had direct access to the original 1995 diagrams, given how precisely the built devices match.
- The Scribe's notebook stock matching the original King in Yellow copy suggests ongoing, deliberate reproduction of the book.

## Status

**Partially resolved** — both devices are now known to be real and have been destroyed, but who built them and why remains open.
