## What Happened

**Session 1:** At [Washington Square Park](/locations/washington-square-park/), a child-sized busker in a mask danced to a looping dirge, dragging a paper dragon through the air with unnervingly precise movements. See: [The Busking Clown](/moments/busking-clown/).

**Session 2:** Two independent developments tie directly back to this image:
- [Thomas Manuel's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/thomas-manuel/) basement painting collection includes a piece depicting **the exact same scene** — the clown in Washington Square Park, trailing a white paper dragon. See [Manuel's Paintings](/mysteries/manuels-paintings/).
- [Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) — who saw the original busker and noticed nothing unusual — came home to find a **white origami paper dragon** sitting on his own front doorstep in White Plains. He destroyed it (crumpled and discarded) before it could be examined. See [Dragon on the Doorstep](/moments/dragon-on-the-doorstep/).

## Why It's Flagged

- [Agent Mason](/characters/mason/) found the original performance deeply unsettling and couldn't get bystanders to engage or react; [Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/), watching the same act, noticed nothing.
- That same Mortimer — seemingly unaffected — is the one who received a personal, targeted "delivery" of the dragon imagery at his own home, in a different part of the city entirely, with no plausible mundane way for anyone to have followed him there. This suggests the clown/dragon motif is not confined to the park and may be tracking or signaling individual agents regardless of whether they consciously perceived it as unnatural.
- Manuel's painting means the imagery also exists independently in someone else's mind/artwork, predating (or paralleling) the agents' encounter — raising the question of whether Manuel witnessed the same performer, or is depicting something else entirely that merely resembles it.

## Current Theories

- The clown/dragon imagery may be a marker or ability tied to whatever unnatural force is behind Operation ALICE, potentially "tagging" people it has taken an interest in.
- Manuel's paintings could be clairvoyant/automatic — worth investigating whether he paints things before or as they happen elsewhere.

## Status

**Escalating, then quiet.** After Session 2's doorstep intrusion, no further confirmed clown sightings occurred — but see [The Masked Figure](/mysteries/the-masked-figure/), a recurring masked presence throughout Sessions 3–9 that shares the same fixation on masks and precise, deliberate movement. Never confirmed as the same entity, but the resemblance is hard to ignore.
