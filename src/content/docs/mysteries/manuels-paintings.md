## What Happened

In [Thomas Manuel's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/thomas-manuel/) basement studio, alongside a blank canvas titled "My Great Work" and three pails of linseed oil marked for pickup by "Sami/Esther," [Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) found three finished paintings:

1. A haunting image of a white-faced specter standing on a rug at the top of a staircase, in the midst of a conflagration (fire). The rug is a strange, aquamarine color.
2. A young man with blond hair, wearing a hospital gown, duplicated in a mirror — his mirror-twin deformed and muscular, with a misshapen head.
3. [Washington Square Park](/locations/washington-square-park/): a child-sized clown in yellow and blue cavorts, trailing a white paper dragon — matching [The Clown](/mysteries/the-clown/) from Session 1.

## Why It's Flagged

- Painting 3 depicts an event Manuel should have no ordinary way of knowing about in that specific detail — the agents haven't publicized the clown sighting.
- The imagery across all three (a specter mid-fire, a deformed mirror double, the clown) suggests either clairvoyance, exposure to the same unnatural source, or direct involvement.
- These paintings disturbed Mortimer enough that he immediately escalated to cuffing and grilling Manuel and forcibly relocating him.

## Current Theories

- Manuel may be unknowingly (or knowingly) channeling visions related to the case.
- The "Sami/Esther" linseed oil pickup could connect him to a wider circle with knowledge of these events.

## Status

**Open.** In Session 5, the team found the linseed oil pails gone from the basement studio but the paintings and blank canvas otherwise undisturbed; Mason considered taking the large canvas but didn't. The paintings, along with the rest of the building, were destroyed in Session 9's demolition — they were never professionally analyzed or removed for the case file.
