## What Happened

Over dinner at [Silvia's Soul Food Restaurant](/locations/operation-alice/silvias-soul-food/), [Mason's](/characters/mason/) partner Gloria said something strange and a little unsettling to him. He kept the conversation light regardless — but afterward isn't certain he actually heard her say it. See: [Dinner with Gloria](/moments/operation-alice/dinner-with-gloria/).

## Why It's Flagged

- Mason's uncertainty about whether the moment even happened as he remembers it is itself notable — it echoes the Mortimer/Mason perception mismatch from [The Clown](/mysteries/the-clown/), where one agent perceives something unnatural and doubts creep in about what was actually real.

## Current Theories

- None yet — content of what Gloria said hasn't been pinned down or recorded.
- May or may not be connected to [The Gloria Photograph](/mysteries/the-gloria-photograph/), an unrelated but equally unsettling incident involving Gloria found later in the Night Floors.
