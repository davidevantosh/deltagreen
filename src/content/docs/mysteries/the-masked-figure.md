## What Happened

A masked figure in white shoes, carrying a briefcase, was first glimpsed by Mortimer rushing past the open bathroom door in Wright's apartment (Session 3) — the room was empty and had no other exit. Similar figures recur throughout the case:

- A masked figure delivered $10,000 in cash to Marlowe's doorstep before vanishing down an alley (Session 6).
- Marlowe glimpsed gold and silver cloth snaking out of a doorway in the Night Floors; Murphy found the room behind it empty (Session 8).
- Murphy saw a lone figure clothed in gold and silver, near the front of an empty theater, beginning to remove a strange white mask through a projection room window — the projector shorted out before the mask came off (Session 8).
- Immediately after the Macallister Building's destruction, Mortimer spotted a masked figure in white shoes disappearing down a nearby alley one final time. The team gave chase; the figure was gone, but left behind an old-fashioned gramophone that started playing on its own (Session 9).

## Why It's Flagged

This figure appears consistently associated with masks, formal or archaic dress, and a sense of being just barely missed — never caught, never fully seen. The *Maude Goes to the Masked Ball* storybook found in Session 7 describes a near-identical "strange prince in gold wearing a white mask," whose own unmasked face is torn out of the book and never shown.

## Current Theories

- May be the same entity referenced as "the new king" in the [masquerade invitation](/evidence/the-invitation/) found in the Night Floors.
- Possibly connected to the original [busking clown](/mysteries/the-clown/) from Session 1 — both involve masks and precise, deliberate movement — though nothing directly confirms the link.

## Status

**Open.** Never caught, never unmasked, never explained. Survived the destruction of the Macallister Building.
