## What Happened

While preparing for their second Night Floors excursion, M-Cell asked handler Agent Marcus about "Operation BRISTOL" — a name that had come up in the course of their investigation. Delta Green has **no record of any such operation.** The team was visibly spooked by this. Marcus warned them to play it straight and wrap up Operation ALICE cleanly.

## Why It's Flagged

Delta Green not having a record of one of its own operations implies either deliberate erasure, an operation that predates current records, or something conducted entirely outside official channels.

## Status

**Open.** Never explained by Marcus or anyone else. What prompted the team to ask about it in the first place isn't documented in the session notes.
