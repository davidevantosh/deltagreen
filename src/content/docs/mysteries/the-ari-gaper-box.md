## What Happened

While closing a joint bank account with his ex-wife Jennifer, Murphy was told he holds safety deposit box 616 at Chase Manhattan — a box he has no memory of opening. Paperwork requested that day eventually confirmed the box was registered on **10 November 1951** by the law firm Keyes, Norris, Ingalls, and Grant, in the name of **Ari Gaper** — registered on that person's own day of birth. In the campaign's epilogue, weeks after Operation ALICE closed, Murphy finally opened it: inside was a 19th-century jeweled crown. He photographed it and locked it away again.

## Why It's Flagged

"Gaper" is Murphy's own real surname. A box registered in 1951, under someone sharing his name, containing a literal crown, discovered in the same case where a masquerade invitation was found celebrating "the new king" — this is very unlikely to be a coincidence.

## Current Theories

- Ari Gaper is likely a direct relative of Murphy's (father or grandfather, given the 1951 date), suggesting Murphy's family has a hidden, direct connection to whatever succession or hierarchy is unfolding around the Macallister Building and the Night Floors.

## Status

**Partially resolved.** The box's contents are now known, but who Ari Gaper was, why the crown was left for safekeeping, and what it means for Murphy personally remain completely open.
