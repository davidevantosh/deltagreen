## What Happened

While the team waited out the Night Floors' smoking lounge in Session 5, Mason flipped through an antique pornographic book left behind by Mark Roark. Deep into it, he found a photograph depicting Agent Mortimer with Mason's own partner, Gloria — an act neither of them should plausibly have any connection to. Mason dropped the book in shock; Marlowe glimpsed it too. Mason tore the page out and burned it in a nearby alley before heading home, telling no one else.

## Why It's Flagged

Nothing about this fits the timeline or the people involved as anyone understands them. It reads as a Night Floors artifact — something manufactured to unsettle or manipulate Mason specifically — rather than a real record of anything Gloria or Mortimer did.

## Current Theories

- The Night Floors may be capable of generating targeted, personal material about the agents to destabilize them individually — the wall of "bottled" photographs bearing Marlowe's real name (see [The Night Floors](/locations/the-night-floors/)) suggests the location has detailed, unsettling knowledge of the team specific to each member.

## Status

**Open.** Mason has not confronted Mortimer or Gloria about it. No physical evidence remains — the page was destroyed.
