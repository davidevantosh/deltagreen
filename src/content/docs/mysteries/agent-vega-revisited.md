## What Happened

In 1995, a man calling himself "Agent Vega" — real name, he insisted, Michael Witwer, a DEA agent — was found tied to a table deep in the Night Floors, claiming the current year was **2015.** He was swallowed by the floor before the team could learn more.

In Session 17, in the actual year 2015, his name resurfaces: a burner phone recovered from Elias Barbas's kitchen carries messages from **Ophelia Sitri** searching for a missing Michael Witwer, and a voicemail from a DEA agent.

## Why It's Flagged

The 1995 date claim wasn't a mistake, a delusion, or a red herring — it was, apparently, exactly correct, twenty years ahead of when it was said. Whatever pulled him into the floor in 1995 either held him somewhere for two decades, or has some other relationship with time that let his identity resurface at precisely the right moment.

## Current Theories

- The Night Floors (or whatever lies beneath it) may not simply be a spatial anomaly but a temporal one, capable of holding or displacing people across decades.
- Ophelia Sitri's search for him suggests he has allies, or handlers, entirely unconnected to Delta Green.

## Status

**Escalating.** Confirmed alive (or at least, actively searched-for) as of 2015. See also [The Cheat Sheets & Agents L–V](/mysteries/the-cheat-sheets-and-agents-lv/), where his code name resurfaces a second time in the same session block.
