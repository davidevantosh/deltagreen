## What Happened

Barbas's [cheat sheets](/evidence/operation-india-moon/the-cheat-sheets/) — detailed files on every member of M-Cell — also named three other active Delta Green agents entirely unknown to the team:

- **Agent Vega** (Michael Witwer) — see [Agent Vega Revisited](/mysteries/agent-vega-revisited/).
- **Agent Venus** (Geneva Brown)
- **Agent Virgo** (Roger Thelemis)

All three share the same secondary address: **the Boxer Hotel, Boston, room 616.**

## Why It's Flagged

M-Cell has never encountered, or been told about, any of these three agents — yet Barbas had detailed files on them alongside everyone in M-Cell, suggesting they're part of the same wider operation or investigation, unknown to the team's own handlers.

The room number, 616, directly echoes [Murphy's own safety deposit box](/mysteries/the-ari-gaper-box/) at Chase Manhattan — also numbered 616 — discovered at the end of Operation ALICE in 1995.

## Current Theories

- "Room 616" may be a recurring, deliberate marker used by whoever or whatever is behind these cases, rather than a coincidence.
- Agent Vega's presence on this list, twenty years after his disappearance into the Night Floors, strongly suggests he survived in some form and remained operationally active.

## Status

**Open.** The Boxer Hotel and room 616 were never investigated by M-Cell.
