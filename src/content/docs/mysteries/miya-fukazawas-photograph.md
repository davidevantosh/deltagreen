## What Happened

Deep in the Night Floors, the team found a wall of dozens of photographs: blank-faced people in 1930s clothing, each holding an oddly-shaped bottle, each captioned with a name in steady ink — including Asa Daribondi, and several others matching names found elsewhere in the case (E. Moseby, H. Lundine). The final photograph in the sequence shows only an empty dark bottle on the floor. Its caption: **Miya Fukazawa** — Agent Marlowe's real name, known at that point to nobody but herself and, as of that same session, Mason.

Marlowe took the photograph down. As she did, a near-silent voice whispered a line about how fearful a thing it is to fall into the hands of the living God.

## Why It's Flagged

This is not a coincidence of a similar name — it is Marlowe's actual real name, in a decades-old photograph, in a location she couldn't have influenced before ever entering it. Every other name on the wall belongs to someone independently tied to this case's deep history.

## Current Theories

- The empty bottle (versus the full ones implied for the other names) may mean Marlowe hasn't yet been "claimed" the way the others were — or that she's slated for something the others already went through.

## Status

**Open.** Not discussed with the rest of the team beyond what they directly witnessed. Marlowe kept the photograph.
