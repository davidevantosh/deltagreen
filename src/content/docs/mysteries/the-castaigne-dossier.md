## What Happened

After purchasing the Hygromanteia from the hidden bookshop in 1995, Calvin (Agent Mason) spent the following two decades obsessively researching the Castaigne family — first encountered as the Night Floors' night manager, [Castaigne](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/castaigne/), and later as multiple generations' worth of signatures found inside a book in Michelle Vanfitz's library.

By 2015, this research directly intersects with Operation INDIA MOON: **Esther Samigina**, glimpsed watching Calvin through his own bathroom mirror in Session 18, shares her surname with a demon from the Ars Goetia — and Calvin separately recalled "Sami," a name from a linseed oil pickup note found in Thomas Manuel's basement studio in 1995.

## Why It's Flagged

Two decades of one agent's private, self-directed research quietly converging with an active operation — without Delta Green's knowledge or involvement — suggests Calvin may know more about the underlying pattern connecting these cases than anyone else on the team, including things he hasn't shared.

## Status

**Open.** The full contents of Calvin's twenty years of research were never shared with the rest of M-Cell or documented on this wiki.
