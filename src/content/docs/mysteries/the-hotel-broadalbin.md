## What Happened

First surfaced as a slip of stationery stapled to a city permit (Session 5), then again as the last known address of missing 1933 salesman [Mark Roark](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/mark-roark/) (Session 6), and confirmed via a 1928 photograph as the hotel where architect [Asa Daribondi](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/asa-daribondi/) once lived — located near Madison Square Park, based on the Eternal Light Flagstaff visible in a reflected window.

Despite this, direct searches have found nothing:
- Mason's internet search for it turned up no results (Session 6).
- The team spent hours physically searching the area around Madison Square Park for any trace of it and found nothing — "a dead end" (Session 9).

## Why It's Flagged

A hotel with no listed street address, referenced across records spanning 1928 through the present, tied to at least two people connected to the Night Floors — yet it cannot be located by any conventional means, even by agents standing where it should be.

## Current Theories

- The Hotel Broadalbin may not exist in any conventionally accessible sense — possibly a Night Floors-adjacent or historical-echo location, similar to how the Macallister's roof door doesn't lead to a roof.

## Status

**Open.** No location has ever been found.
