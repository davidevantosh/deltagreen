## What Happened

Marlowe first found the sign scrawled on a napkin hidden behind a sheet in Wright's apartment (Session 5) — exposing it filled the room with melancholy, anxiety, and paranoia. She and, later, Mortimer and Murphy were badly affected; Mason and Mabel initially were not. A second instance was found on a [1905 newspaper scrap](/evidence/operation-alice/yellow-sign-newspaper/) deep in the Night Floors (Session 7), where showing it around caused every agent present to involuntarily ask "Tell me, have you seen the Yellow Sign?" without realizing they'd spoken.

## Effects Observed

- A compulsion to "share" the sign with others, growing stronger the longer it goes unsatisfied.
- Marlowe satisfied the compulsion by graffitiing the sign on a building wall (Session 5).
- Mason satisfied it by scrawling it into his novel manuscript and sending it to his literary agent, Morgan Goldstein (Session 8) — he reported feeling better afterward.
- Mabel, unable or unwilling to share it, spiraled into a full catatonic fugue state by Session 8, losing an entire day (Session 9).
- Mabel's wife Teri left him a tape recording ending with her shouting "HAVE YOU SEEN IT?" — suggesting the compulsion, or the sign itself, may be transmissible even secondhand.

## Current Theories

- The sign functions as a self-propagating psychological hazard: once seen, it demands to be passed on, and resisting it is actively harmful.
- Directly connects to *Le Roi en Jaune* (*The King in Yellow*) — the same red book Wright purchased and Mason ultimately read in full.

## Status

**Active and spreading.** As of Session 9, the sign now exists inside at least one literary manuscript in general circulation (Mason's, with Goldstein) and has apparently reached Teri, who was never directly present for any in-game exposure the agents witnessed.
