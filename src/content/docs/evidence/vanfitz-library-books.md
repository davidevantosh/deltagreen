## Description

Books found across two visits to Michelle Vanfitz's impossible library:

- ***A World Without Doors*** by Emeline F. Fitzroy (1936, Torbitt Books) — a girl named Abby reaches a magical land by drowning herself, battles a force called the Phantom to free people from "the dance," and ultimately becomes the Phantom herself to keep the realm from collapsing.
- **English-Tartessian Dictionary** (1931) — defines an entire constructed language, "Tartessian," with a unique alphabet.
- An oversized anatomical text with a diagrammed horse and gibberish text, signed by "A. Chastaigne," "G. Castaigne," and "H. Castaigne."
- *A History of the Russo-Germanic Hegemony*, 1911–1921.
- ***Maude Goes to the Masked Ball*** — a children's book whose illustrations depict [Thomas Manuel's locket](/evidence/manuels-locket/) and a masked prince whose unmasked face is torn out of the book's final page.

## Significance

*A World Without Doors* is a striking thematic parallel to Abigail Wright's own situation. The Castaigne signatures suggest the name spans generations. See [Michelle Vanfitz](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/michelle-vanfitz/) and [The Night Floors](/locations/the-night-floors/).
