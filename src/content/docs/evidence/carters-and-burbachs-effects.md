## Description

Wallets carried by two "dead" marionettes found in the Night Floors — torn open by buckshot, bleeding red tissue paper. Contain genuine, brand-new-looking 1950s New York identity cards for [Eric K. Carter and Ronald Burbach](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/carter-and-burbach/), along with period-accurate cash, bus passes, and other effects, despite both men having been murdered on 9 September 1955.

## Significance

The items look freshly made, not decades-aged, despite belonging to men murdered forty years before the current case. The Night Floors appears to preserve — or replay — this history rather than simply record it.
