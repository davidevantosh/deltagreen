## Description

A cardboard box found amid the "ordered chaos" of Wright's apartment, marked **"EVIDENCE, FBI WHITE PLAINS, WRIGHT-A, 10-AUG-95."**

## Contents

- Four Polaroid cameras
- 100 packs of film
- Latex gloves
- Evidence bags
- Stickers
- Sealing tape
- A manifest list for the box's contents

## Chain of Custody

- Found during the official Friday morning visit to the apartment, Session 1.

## Significance

The label suggests this box was staged or prepared by the FBI's White Plains office specifically for this case, on the same day Wright went missing — timing worth scrutinizing. The volume of photographic equipment (4 cameras, 100 packs of film) is unusual for a standard evidence kit.
