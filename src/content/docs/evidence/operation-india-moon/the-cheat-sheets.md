## Description

Detailed files found in Barbas's house listing every member of M-Cell by their real name, home address, and every personal Bond — including details concealed from Delta Green itself.

## Significance

Ended twenty years of code-name anonymity within the team in a single afternoon — everyone now knows everyone else completely. Also lists three other active Delta Green agents entirely unknown to M-Cell: **Agent Vega** (Michael Witwer), **Agent Venus** (Geneva Brown), and **Agent Virgo** (Roger Thelemis) — all sharing the Boxer Hotel, room 616, as a secondary address. See [The Cheat Sheets & Agents L–V](/mysteries/the-cheat-sheets-and-agents-lv/).
