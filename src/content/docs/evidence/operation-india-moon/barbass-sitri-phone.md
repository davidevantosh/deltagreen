## Description

A burner phone recovered by Marlowe from Barbas's kitchen, marked with a demonic seal for **Sitri.** Contains messages from **Ophelia Sitri** searching for a missing Michael Witwer, and a voicemail from a DEA agent.

## Significance

Directly ties Barbas to [Agent Vega / Michael Witwer](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/agent-vega/) — the man swallowed by the Night Floors in 1995 while insisting the year was 2015. See [Agent Vega Revisited](/mysteries/agent-vega-revisited/).
