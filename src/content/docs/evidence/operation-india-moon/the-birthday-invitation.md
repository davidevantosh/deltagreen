## Description

An ornate FedEx invitation to a birthday party for "Richard Zielony" at Gateway Bridges Restaurant, Dorchester, embossed with a green triangle. Tearing it open reveals a hidden handwriting comparison sample and a gold Purson seal.

## Significance

A deliberately disguised Delta Green summons, using the same Purson seal motif first found in Abigail Wright's apartment in 1995. Saying "Purson" aloud triggered a fire alarm at Dutch's office — a small, unexplained instance of the seal's power carrying real-world effect.
