## Description

Found upstairs in Barbas's house — a strange, automated writing/binding machine, matching the name ("The Scribe") of one of the two impossible mechanical diagrams found on napkins in Abigail Wright's apartment during Operation ALICE, twenty years earlier.

## Significance

Confirms "The Scribe" from the 1995 diagrams was a real, buildable device — and that Barbas had one, alongside a supply of identical red leather notebooks matching the stock of the original *Le Roi en Jaune*. Likely used to mass-produce or hand-copy occult texts.
