## Description

Recovered from Barbas's body after he was killed: a copper key to "room 616," a security card for Dorchester House, a pill case of rotten baby teeth, and hand-copied pages of *The King in Yellow.*

## Significance

The "room 616" key is a striking echo of Murphy's own safety deposit box 616 at Chase Manhattan from the end of Operation ALICE — a repeated number across two completely separate threads, twenty years apart.
