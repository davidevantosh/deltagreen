## Description

An old, undelivered postcard found on Elias Barbas's porch, addressed cryptically to someone referred to only as "M," mentioning "Abigail" and a man named "Moseby."

## Significance

A direct, unexplained callback to Operation ALICE — [Abigail Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/) and [Moseby](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/moseby/) were both central to events at the Macallister Building twenty years earlier and 300 miles away. Confirms Barbas's connection to that case predates M-Cell's own involvement with him.
