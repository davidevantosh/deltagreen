## Description

A box of identical, blank red leather notebooks found alongside [the Scribe](/evidence/operation-india-moon/the-scribe/) — the same stock as the original *Le Roi en Jaune* copy recovered during Operation ALICE.

## Significance

Suggests active, ongoing production of new copies of *The King in Yellow*, twenty years after and 300 miles from where the original was found.
