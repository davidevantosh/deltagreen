## Description

A police report filed by Abigail Wright, found by Murphy during an FBI records check. Dated **30 August 1994**: Wright reported being mugged by a large, older man in a silver robe and mask near the Macallister Building's roof door. The man demanded to know "where the tunnels are," knocked her down, rifled through her possessions, and fled.

## Significance

Establishes that Wright — and the building's unnatural elements — were already entangled a full year before her August 1995 disappearance, and that at least one other party was actively searching for something ("the tunnels") within or beneath the building.
