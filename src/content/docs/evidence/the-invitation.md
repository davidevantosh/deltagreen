## Description

An invitation on finely pressed vellum, delivered by a small clockwork figure deep in the Night Floors, addressed to **"Ian Frederick Decraig, Prisoner 125101."** It reads: "Join us at the palace. A masquerade to celebrate the new king. Come to dine. Come to dance. Come."

## Significance

Confirms an active, ongoing event — a coronation or succession of "the new king" — organized within the Night Floors. The identity of "Ian Frederick Decraig" is unknown.
