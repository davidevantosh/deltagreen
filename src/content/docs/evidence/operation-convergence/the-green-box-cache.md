A Delta Green weapons and equipment cache found in a storage unit at the local Handi-Mart, including a warning note: "PLEASE exercise caution. We don't want a Waco."

## Significance

Standard Delta Green field logistics — but the explicit warning against a repeat of Waco suggests this wasn't the first time an operation here required a heavy hand.
