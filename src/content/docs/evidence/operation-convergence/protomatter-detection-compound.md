## Description

A compound developed by [Dr. Bennett](/npcs/bonds/dr-bennett/) that reveals protomatter contamination on contact. Used to confirm the scale of the problem in Groversville: Murphy's torso was covered in invisible surgical scarring, and the town's own tap water tested positive.

## Significance

Confirmed the protomatter contamination extended well beyond Billy Ray Spivey — into the water supply and, unknowingly, into at least one Delta Green agent. When the source site was neutralized, all protomatter in agents' bodies went inert simultaneously, nearly killing Murphy.
