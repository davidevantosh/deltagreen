## Description

A golden seal reading **PURSON**, found mounted on the wall of Abigail Wright's apartment by Mortimer. Removing it triggered a brief, disorienting blast of noise that resolved into mundane street sounds. Mason identified it as a demonic seal from the Ars Goetia.

## Significance

Purson is described in occult reference material as a Great King of Hell, associated with hidden knowledge of the past, present, and future. A matching ritual circle — complete with a smoking brass vessel and a knife-etched pentagram — was later found active deep in the Night Floors in Session 8, alongside the sound of someone reciting a summoning invocation for Purson. Someone or something within the Night Floors is actively working with this seal.
