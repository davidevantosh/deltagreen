## Description

Records requested by Murphy from Chase Manhattan Bank regarding safety deposit box 616 — a box he has no memory of opening, discovered while closing a joint account with his ex-wife Jennifer.

## Significance

Per the paperwork received weeks later, the box was registered by the law firm Keyes, Norris, Ingalls, and Grant on **10 November 1951**, in the name of **Ari Gaper** — registered on that person's day of birth, in their own name. Opened in the epilogue: contains a 19th-century jeweled crown. See [The Ari Gaper Box](/mysteries/the-ari-gaper-box/).
