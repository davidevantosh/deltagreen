## Description

A vintage military surplus walkie-talkie backpack unit, found by Mortimer at 10:55 PM, attached to the wall by resin. Powering it on produced static, then a recording of a voice reciting words.

## Significance

On a second listen, the unit played back a recording of Mortimer's own voice interrogating "Mister Manuel," and Mason's voice warning "you know those things will kill you." Neither agent had said these exact words at that point in the timeline. In Session 4, the unit spontaneously activated on its own, with a live voice asking "Hello? Who is this?" — Mortimer answered as "Alan." Presumed destroyed along with the building in Session 9.
