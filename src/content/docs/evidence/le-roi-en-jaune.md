## Description

A small, leather-bound red book in French, found by Mortimer at Michelle Vanfitz's bedside — the same "Red Book" Abigail Wright purchased per the [Farsi receipt](/evidence/farsi-receipt/). Vanfitz attacked the team when Mortimer took it, claiming "Abby" had given it to her. Mason, fluent in French, identified it on sight: *Le Roi en Jaune* — *The King in Yellow.*

## Significance

Purchased from a hidden occult bookshop (see [The Bookshop](/locations/the-bookshop/)), whose proprietor confirmed Wright bought it there and claims not to know its publisher. A second copy was obtained in Session 9. Agent Mason read the book directly in Session 9 — described only as "his mind is opened." Effects unknown but presumed serious.
