## Description

A 19th-century crown — velvet, jewels, and gold — found inside [safety deposit box 616](/evidence/murphys-mystery-key/) when Murphy finally opened it in the campaign's epilogue, weeks after the Macallister Building's destruction. Murphy photographed it with a polaroid before locking it away again.

## Significance

The box was registered in 1951 in the name of Ari Gaper — apparently a relative of Murphy's, given the shared surname — on that person's own day of birth. A crown, paired with the masquerade invitation celebrating "the new king" found in the Night Floors, strongly suggests Murphy's own family has a direct, unexplained connection to whatever hierarchy or succession is unfolding at the heart of this case.
