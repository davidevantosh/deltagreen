## Description

Building blueprints and permits obtained by Mabel and Marlowe from City Hall for $200 in fees. The original building permit is dated **27 May 1921**: the building began as a private residence for Henry M. Lundine (28 May 1886 – 30 Apr 1952), built in classic brownstone style. An addendum dated **2 March 1953** shows the Lundine house being refitted into an apartment building, with the architect listed as A. Darabondi (elsewhere spelled [Asa Daribondi](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/asa-daribondi/)).

## Significance

Establishes a firm construction and conversion timeline for the [Macallister Building](/locations/operation-alice/macallister-building/), and directly ties Daribondi to the building's transformation into its current form.
