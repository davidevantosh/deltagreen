## Description

An old, digitized legal brief for *Antonia Sclara Carter (Widow) v. Eileen Carter*, dated **12 February 1956**, found by Mortimer. Indicates Eric Kristos Carter was murdered in an unnamed New York City hotel in 1955; his wife and mother sued each other over his personal effects and Army pension. The document is a summary only and does not record the case's outcome.

## Significance

Corroborates [Carter and Burbach's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/carter-and-burbach/) 1955 murder and connects it to a hotel setting — likely the same location referenced by the Hotel Broadalbin stationery.
