## Description

A thin, typewritten file describing the **9 September 1955** murder of Ronald Burbach and Eric Carter at an unnamed hotel. Contains a single black-and-white crime scene photograph showing both men laid out in heavy military jackets, the skin of their faces carefully removed to reveal blood and muscle, next to a pile of old gas masks and shotguns. Lists a single person of interest: **Griffith, Virgil U. (White Plains, NY).**

## Significance

Matches the appearance and gear of the "three men in gas masks with shotguns" the team witnessed in the Night Floors, and the identical cache of coats, masks, and a 1954-stamped shotgun found hidden in the bookshop's stacks in Session 9.
