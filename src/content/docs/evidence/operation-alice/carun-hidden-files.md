## Description

Found on Roger Carun's home computer by Marlowe and Mabel. One folder contains a copy of "the play" (an early draft or variant of *Le Roi en Jaune*), rewritten from Carun's own point of view. A second folder contains 64 short stories, each consisting of a single letter repeated hundreds of times — read in order, they spell out a message about a steady hand shaping the world.

## Significance

The phrase is later directly echoed by a manicurist's throwaway comment to Marlowe in Session 5 ("you have amazing hands, so smooth and steady") — suggesting whatever authored Carun's files has a wider reach than his apartment alone.
