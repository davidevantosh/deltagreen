## Description

Found by Mason in a small clearing among the bookshop's bookcases: a shallow wooden crate, recently opened, containing a long leather trench coat, an oversized full-face gas mask, and a recently-oiled pump shotgun — stamped 1954, but looking brand new and fully loaded. Two more identical, unopened crates were stacked beneath it.

## Significance

Matches the description of the gas-masked men seen carrying shotguns in the Night Floors, and the crime scene details from the 1955 [Carter/Burbach murder file](/evidence/operation-alice/carter-burbach-murder-file/). This cache appears to be an active, maintained supply point, not a historical relic.
