## Description

Three library books found by Marlowe and Mabel while researching architect Asa Daribondi:

- *The Devil's Craftsman* by Cavett Monaghan, 1952
- *A Darkness at the Corners* by Elizabeth Ortiz, 1957
- *Architecture, Genius, and Mental Illness* by Dr. Peter Johansson, 1961

## Significance

Establish Daribondi's full biography — see [Asa Daribondi](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/asa-daribondi/) — including a 1928 photograph in *The Devil's Craftsman* showing him outside an art deco hotel with a "B" on each door, reflecting Madison Square Park's Eternal Light Flagstaff in its windows: this confirmed the real-world location of the [Hotel Broadalbin](/mysteries/the-hotel-broadalbin/). Also turned up a 1926 photograph of the Macallister Building featuring [Abigail Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/), decades before her disappearance.
