## Description

A hand-written receipt in Farsi, found in [Abigail Wright's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/) apartment. Reads: **"22 Feb, 3:10pm. A. Wright. Red Book x1."** The business name is torn off, but ends **"-ookshop"** (almost certainly "...Bookshop").

## Chain of Custody

- Found during the Session 2 apartment catalogue.
- Kept on [Agent Murphy's](/characters/murphy/) person along with the other items of interest, rather than bagged with the rest of the catalogued junk.

## Significance

Confirmed Wright purchased *Le Roi en Jaune* — "Red Book x1" — from a hidden bookshop in February, months before her disappearance. The bookshop itself was located and visited in Session 9; see [The Bookshop](/locations/operation-alice/the-bookshop/).
