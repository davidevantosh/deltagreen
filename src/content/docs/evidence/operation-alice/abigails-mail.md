## Description

Mail belonging to [Abigail Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/), originally recovered from her mailbox in Session 1 and reviewed by [Agent Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/) in Session 2. A series of utility bills and late payment reminders dating back to **March**, culminating in termination notices a couple of months ago.

## Significance

Despite the termination notices, water and power are still running in the apartment — someone or something is keeping utilities active without payment. The March timeline lines up with the "late March" expiry dates found on still-fresh food in [Thomas Manuel's fridge](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/thomas-manuel/), suggesting March is a significant point in the case's timeline.
