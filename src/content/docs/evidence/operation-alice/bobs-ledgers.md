## Description

Ledgers stolen from a locked strongbox in [Bob's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/bob/) bookshop while he was distracted fetching a book for Mortimer. Read by Mabel: every entry is nothing but the poem "Jabberwocky" (Lewis Carroll, *Alice in Wonderland*) translated into Farsi, repeated for well over a thousand pages, with no client or transaction information. Entries are dated in French, beginning **"30 Août 1895"** (30 August 1895). The handwriting is identical throughout, the earliest entries in faded brown ink.

## Significance

A single unbroken, century-old ritual or compulsive act, seemingly maintained by one hand — or something using many hands identically — since 1895. The choice of "Jabberwocky," from *Alice in Wonderland*, is a pointed echo of Operation ALICE's own name and of Abigail Wright's situation.
