## Description

$10,000 in cash, found by Marlowe in a paper bag left on her doorstep at 10:30 PM, delivered by a masked figure she glimpsed disappearing down an alley. She injured herself trying to give chase.

## Significance

An unexplained, deliberate payment or bribe directed personally at Marlowe. No demand or explanation accompanied it.
