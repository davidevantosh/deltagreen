## Description

A file describing Ronald James Burbach's arrest on the evening of **1 June 1953** at the American Museum of Natural History for breaking and entering. Burbach admitted nothing. He was released the following week on the direct order of NYPD Commissioner Francis William Holbrooke Adams, for the sole listed reason "decorated veteran." All charges were dropped.

## Significance

The unusual intervention by the police commissioner himself suggests Burbach had connections or protection well beyond an ordinary veteran.
