## Description

A hidden microphone discovered by [Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) under the door of [Abigail Wright's apartment](/locations/operation-alice/macallister-building/). Its wire led across the hallway to the apartment opposite.

## Chain of Custody

- Discovered during the illegal night entry, Session 1.
- Team chose not to trace it further at the time.
- Crushed underfoot by Mortimer the next morning during the official visit with Detective Giuradanda — destroyed before it could be examined or dusted for prints.

## Significance

Confirms someone was actively surveilling Wright before her disappearance. The apartment across the hall is now a location of interest that hasn't been investigated.
