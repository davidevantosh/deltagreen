## Description

Charcoal and ink drawings found on Louis Post's drawing table, examined by Murphy after Post's attack. Hundreds of pages depicting drowned infants, bloated bodies, clawed hands, and umbilical cords wound around necks. Murphy took the topmost drawing as evidence.

## Significance

Disturbing in content and volume, suggesting either deep unnatural exposure or influence. Post's baroque mirror, found alongside the drawings, drew Murphy's attention in a way he had to consciously resist.
