## Description

Mail recovered from Thomas Manuel's mailbox by Mortimer and reviewed by Mason. Follows the same pattern as [Abigail's Mail](/evidence/abigails-mail/) — late bills starting in March, culminating in termination notices — plus letters from his parents, [Elaine and Arthur Manuel](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/the-manuels/), desperately trying to reach him.

## Significance

The letters reveal that on **31 March 1995**, after dark, the Manuels attempted to contact Thomas directly at the Macallister Building and were turned away by "a man with a suitcase and a large grey dog," who told them Thomas no longer lived there. This is the clearest evidence yet that whatever controls access to the building was actively managing Thomas Manuel's disappearance from the outside world well before the agents' investigation began.
