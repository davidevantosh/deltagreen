## Description

A single slip of elegant cream-colored stationery for the **Hotel Broadalbin**, New York City (no address listed), stapled to a city permit obtained by Mabel and Marlowe at City Hall. A handwritten note in long-faded ballpoint reads as an account of seeing "the rooms" one night at dusk.

## Significance

The Hotel Broadalbin is confirmed, via later research, to be the hotel where architect [Asa Daribondi](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/asa-daribondi/) lived after moving to New York in 1921 — located near Madison Square Park. Direct searches for the hotel itself, both online (Session 6) and in person (Session 9), turned up nothing. See [The Hotel Broadalbin](/mysteries/the-hotel-broadalbin/).
