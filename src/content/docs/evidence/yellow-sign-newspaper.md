## Description

A scrap of newspaper found on a wall deep in the Night Floors by Mabel, bearing the Yellow Sign scrawled on it. The paper is identified as coming from the **Chicago Tribune, 20 August 1905.**

## Significance

As Mabel showed this to the team, every agent present involuntarily asked, in turn, "Tell me, have you seen the Yellow Sign?" — without realizing they'd spoken. This is the same symbol Marlowe first found in Wright's apartment in Session 5, now confirmed to have circulated in the real world as early as 1905.
