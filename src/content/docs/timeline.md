A chronological reconstruction of the campaign, built from dated evidence and testimony recovered during each investigation. In-session events (the agents' own timeline) are marked separately from historical dates uncovered through research.

## Historical Background

- **30 Août (Aug) 1895** — First entry in [Bob's stolen ledgers](/evidence/operation-alice/bobs-ledgers/): the poem "Jabberwocky," translated into Farsi, in ink that would go on to repeat identically for a thousand pages and a hundred years.
- **20 Aug 1905** — Date of origin for the [Yellow Sign newspaper scrap](/evidence/operation-alice/yellow-sign-newspaper/) found in the Night Floors, published in the Chicago Tribune.
- **27 May 1921** — Original building permit filed: the future Macallister Building begins as a private residence for Henry M. Lundine. See [Macallister Building Permits](/evidence/operation-alice/macallister-building-permits/).
- **1926** — A photograph of the Macallister Building is taken. It includes Abigail Wright, who will not disappear for another 69 years.
- **1928** — A photograph places architect Asa Daribondi outside the Hotel Broadalbin, near Madison Square Park.
- **1933** — Mark Roark, a door-to-door salesman, is reported missing. Last known address: the Hotel Broadalbin.
- **1943** — Eric K. Carter is drafted into the US Army.
- **1944** — Ronald Burbach volunteers for the Marines.
- **1947** — Asa Daribondi begins his suspected string of child murders.
- **1950** — Daribondi vanishes without a trace, leaving a drowned five-year-old victim and a log book tracking deaths back to 1921.
- **2 Mar 1953** — Addendum to the building permit: the Lundine house is refitted into the Macallister Building's current apartment form. Architect: Asa Daribondi.
- **1 Jun 1953** — Ronald Burbach is arrested for breaking and entering at the American Museum of Natural History; released a week later by direct order of the NYPD Commissioner, no charges filed. See [Burbach's Arrest File](/evidence/operation-alice/burbach-arrest-file/).
- **9 Sep 1955** — Eric K. Carter and Ronald Burbach are murdered together at an unnamed hotel, their faces skinned, found beside a pile of gas masks and shotguns. See [Carter/Burbach Murder File](/evidence/operation-alice/carter-burbach-murder-file/).
- **12 Feb 1956** — A legal brief records a lawsuit between Carter's widow and mother over his estate. See [Carter Estate Legal Brief](/evidence/operation-alice/carters-legal-brief/).
- **2 Sep 1960** — Asa Daribondi is declared legally dead by his mother, a decade after his disappearance.
- **10 Nov 1951** — A safety deposit box (616, Chase Manhattan) is registered by the law firm Keyes, Norris, Ingalls, and Grant, in the name of Ari Gaper — on that person's own day of birth. See [The Ari Gaper Box](/mysteries/the-ari-gaper-box/).
- **30 Aug 1994** — Abigail Wright is mugged near the Macallister's roof door by a masked man demanding to know "where the tunnels are." See [Wright's 1994 Police Report](/evidence/operation-alice/wrights-1994-police-report/).

## 1995 — The Lead-Up

- **22 Feb 1995** — Abigail Wright purchases *Le Roi en Jaune* ("Red Book x1") from a hidden bookshop. See [Farsi Receipt](/evidence/operation-alice/farsi-receipt/).
- **20 Mar 1995** — Cable repairman David Langford enters the Macallister Building to service a junction box and becomes trapped in the Night Floors.
- **c. mid-to-late Mar 1995** — Building-wide anomalies begin: tenants stop making calls or spending money; utility bills across multiple apartments start lapsing. See [The March Timeline](/mysteries/the-march-timeline/).
- **31 Mar 1995** — Thomas Manuel's parents are turned away from the building after dark by "a man with a suitcase and a large grey dog," who tells them Thomas no longer lives there. See [Thomas Manuel's Mail](/evidence/operation-alice/manuels-mail/).

## 1995 — Operation ALICE

- **Thu 10 Aug 1995** — M-Cell is briefed on Operation ALICE at Washington Square Park. Wright's apartment is illegally entered that night. *(Session 1)*
- **Fri 11 Aug 1995** — Official visit to Wright's apartment with NYPD. Full-day apartment catalogue begins; Thomas Manuel is interrogated and relocated. *(Sessions 1–2)*
- **Sat 12 Aug 1995** — The Purson seal is found. The team first enters the Night Floors. The Yellow Sign is not yet found (that's Sunday) but Michelle Vanfitz is confronted and shot this night. *(Sessions 3, 4, 6)*
- **Sun 13 Aug 1995** — Research day. The Yellow Sign is discovered in Wright's apartment. City Hall records and Chase Manhattan's mystery safety deposit box both surface. *(Session 5)*
- **Mon 14 Aug 1995** — Deep-records research day (Carter, Burbach, Daribondi). Second Night Floors excursion begins; Louis Post attacks the team. *(Sessions 6–7)*
- **Tue 15 Aug 1995** — The team escapes the Night Floors after 12+ hours inside; collective breakdown. David Langford is handed off to Delta Green the next morning. *(Session 8)*
- **Wed 16 Aug 1995** — Mabel loses the day to a fugue state. The team locates and raids the hidden bookshop. *(Session 9)*
- **Thu 17 Aug 1995** — The Macallister Building is destroyed. Operation ALICE is formally closed; M-Cell is stood down. *(Session 9)*
- **A few weeks later** — Agent Murphy opens safety deposit box 616 at Chase Manhattan and finds a 19th-century jeweled crown inside. *(Epilogue)*

## 1969

- **1969** — The "Manchester Shadow Team," a Delta Green operation, ends with all four of its agents dead within two weeks of each other. See [The Manchester Shadow Team Files](/evidence/the-baughman-sweep/manchester-shadow-team-files/).

## January 1999 — The Baughman Sweep

- **January 1999** — Delta Green field agent Clyde Baughman dies of a heart attack. Mason and Murphy sweep his apartment and Catskills cabin, discovering and destroying "That Which Was Marlene." *(Session 10)*

## September 2000 — Operation CONVERGENCE

- **September 2000** — M-Cell investigates Billy Ray Spivey's protomatter-altered muscle tissue in Groversville, Tennessee, uncovering a Mi-Go breeding operation. Murphy nearly dies; a rival faction extracts the remaining evidence. *(Sessions 11–13)*

## 2000–2015 — Twenty Years

- **2001** — Dick Byrne is appointed U.S. Marshal for the Southern District of New York; passes over Reggie for Chief Deputy, ending their friendship.
- **2007** — Des Butler's best friend Barry Romano dies of an apparent heroin overdose.
- **2010** — Des and Teri divorce.
- **c. 2000s–2010s** — Calvin's literary agent Morgan Goldstein is "disappeared" by Delta Green after publishing Yellow Sign-inflected work; institutionalized at Dorchester House.
*(Session 14)*

## August–September 2015 — Operation INDIA MOON

- **28 Aug 2015** — All five agents receive the Purson-sealed birthday invitation summoning them to Boston. *(Session 15)*
- **1 Sep 2015** — M-Cell reunites for the first time in 20 years; briefed on Operation INDIA MOON by "Agent Exeter" (Dr. Elias Barbas). *(Session 15)*
- **June 2015** — Per his personnel file, Barbas's breakdown and administrative leave begins — the likely starting point for his activity as "Agent Exeter." *(Referenced, Session 16)*
- **Early September 2015** — Dorchester House is investigated; the missing operatives, the mummified child, and the cryptic postcard are all found. *(Session 16)*
- **Mid-September 2015** — First raid on Barbas's house; "the Lion" critically wounds Dick Byrne. *(Session 17)*
- **Mid-September 2015, days later** — Second raid: the Lion is destroyed, Barbas is killed, his house is burned, and M-Cell's cheat sheets are found. Operation INDIA MOON is closed. *(Session 18)*
