## Role

Delta Green handler who activated Mason and Murphy for the off-books cleanup of Clyde Baughman's apartment and effects following his death. Briefed them at the post office headquarters with a strict 48-hour window before Baughman's children arrived.

## Session Notes

- **Session 10:** Ordered the agents to finish the job after they reported what they found at the cabin — return, confirm the entity's destruction, and remove the remains. Advised against opening the sealed Delta Green envelope found in the footlocker: "maybe next time."
