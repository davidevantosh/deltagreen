## Role

26-year-old painter. Lives in the apartment directly across the hall from [Abigail Wright](/npcs/abigail-wright/) — the [hidden microphone](/evidence/hidden-microphone/) found in Session 1 was wired to his apartment.

## Session 2 Notes

- Interrogated by [Agent Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/), who confiscated his cassette tapes. Manuel claimed to genuinely have no idea why the microphone was wired to his place.
- Showed Mortimer his basement painting room, which contained a large blank canvas titled **"My Great Work,"** three pails of linseed oil marked for pickup by "Sami/Esther," and three unsettling finished paintings — see [Manuel's Paintings](/mysteries/manuels-paintings/).
- Disturbed by what he found, Mortimer cuffed and grilled Manuel, then ordered him to vacate his apartment and stay with his parents. Manuel agreed but was slow to leave, and Mortimer/Mason had to return later to haul him out.
- [Mason](/characters/mason/) got his parents' phone number and left them a message.
- His apartment, once vacated, was found austere and hotel-like, with a fridge stocked with meat and dairy that was still fresh despite expiry dates from **late March** — and then sealed with crime tape.
