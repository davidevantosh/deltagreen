## Role

Neighbor of [Agent Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/) (real name Miya) in Chinatown.

## Session Notes

- **Session 2:** Tried to say hello; was coldly rebuffed.
- **Session 6:** Came by with cookies while Marlowe was recovering from a fall; Marlowe told her to "fuck off."
- **Session 8:** Came by again with cookies; Marlowe answered the door and slapped the plate out of her hands, leaving her standing in shock.

## Notes

The relationship has deteriorated sharply and one-sidedly across the case — Peggy's overtures have gone from friendly to fearful. Marlowe was later overheard, alone in her apartment, listening to Peggy laughing with another neighbor next door.
