## Role

[Agent Mortimer's](/characters/mortimer/) work partner outside Delta Green in 1995. Mortimer covered a deposition hearing for him that year, and the two went for beers afterward.

## Session Notes

- **Session 5/6:** Mortimer attended Reggie's deposition hearing in court on his behalf.
- **Session 14:** In 2001, when Mortimer was appointed U.S. Marshal for the Southern District of New York, he passed Reggie over for the Chief Deputy role in favor of Bass Reeves — ending the friendship.

## Open Threads

- The two have not reconciled as of 2015.
