## Role

Ex-wife of [Agent Murphy](/characters/murphy/) (real name Dutch Gaper). The two co-own a dog, Gonzo, who lives with her.

## Session Notes

- **Session 5:** Asked Murphy to drop Gonzo off at her old house; her friend Ricardo answered the door.
- **Session 6:** Called Murphy asking him to close out a joint bank account — the visit that led to the discovery of his mysterious safety deposit box. See [The Ari Gaper Box](/mysteries/the-ari-gaper-box/).
