## Role

Former literary agent to [Agent Mason](/characters/mason/) (real name Calvin Cooper).

## Session Notes

- **Session 5:** Pitched Mason on ghost-writing a new novel in Roger Carun's Nightsea series, after Carun's editor Carmen Wagner planned to file a missing person report on him.
- **Session 8:** Unknowingly received Mason's manuscript with the Yellow Sign scrawled into it.
- **Session 14:** Sometime in the years after, "disappeared" by Delta Green after publishing one too many Yellow Sign-inflected works — Calvin took it as a warning and reined in his own writing.
- **Session 16:** Found alive and institutionalized at Dorchester House, twenty years later. Recognized Calvin on the facility tour, but pretended not to know him.
