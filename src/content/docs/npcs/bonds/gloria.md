## Role

Live-in partner of [Agent Mason](/characters/mason/) (real name Calvin Cooper) in Harlem.

## Session Notes

- **Session 1:** Waiting up for Mason when he got home; the two arranged a dinner date.
- **Session 2:** Met Mason for dinner at Silvia's Soul Food Restaurant; said something strange he wasn't sure he actually heard. See [Gloria's Strange Words](/mysteries/glorias-strange-words/).
- **Session 5:** Mason is noted to have spent "a pleasant evening" with her before visiting his literary agent the next morning.
