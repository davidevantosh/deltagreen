## Role

Artist. Subject of [Operation ALICE](/cases/operation-alice/) — reported missing, prompting the FBI missing person case that brought M-Cell in.

## Known Details

- Resident of the [Macallister Building](/locations/macallister-building/), a non-profit offering subsidised rent to artists.
- Her apartment was found in a state of "ordered chaos" when Mortimer and Marlowe searched it.
- A hidden microphone was found planted under her apartment door, wired to the apartment across the hallway — someone was surveilling her.
- FBI evidence box logged for her case includes four Polaroid cameras, 100 packs of film, gloves, evidence bags, and a manifest.
