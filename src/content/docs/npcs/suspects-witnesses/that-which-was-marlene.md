## Role

Once [Clyde Baughman's](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/clyde-baughman/) wife, Marlene — transformed by an unspecified "awful demonic prayer" into something inhuman and enslaved, then imprisoned by her own husband in his Catskills cabin's septic tank for years.

## Session Notes

- **Session 10:** Found pleading for help from inside the sealed tank. What emerged was no longer recognizably human. A brutal fight followed, ending with her burned alive by Mason.

## Open Threads

- What she was "enslaved" to, and what prayer or ritual transformed her, were never explained.
