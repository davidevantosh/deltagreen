## Role

Proprietor of the hidden bookshop where [Abigail Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/) purchased her copy of *Le Roi en Jaune*. Tall, meticulously dressed, a waistcoat barely containing a paunch, smoking a spiced cigarette, gap-toothed, small silver folding glasses.

## Session Notes

- **Session 9:** Effusively welcoming to the team. Confirmed Abigail Wright bought the red book from him, though he claims not to know its publisher. Led Mortimer an "impossible distance" through the store's stacks to a tiny trapdoor "basement" — bare stone walls, dirt floor — to fetch a second copy. While the light was briefly switched off down there, Mortimer saw an unidentified hand pass the book to Bob in the dark. The team stole his ledgers while he was distracted with Mortimer.
