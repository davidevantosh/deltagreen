## Role

Artist. Subject of [Operation ALICE](/cases/operation-alice/) — reported missing, prompting the FBI missing person case that brought M-Cell in.

## Known Details

- Resident of the [Macallister Building](/locations/macallister-building/), a non-profit offering subsidised rent to artists, operated by a nonprofit called ARTLIFE.
- Her apartment was found in a state of "ordered chaos," with a hidden microphone planted under her door.
- Bought a copy of *Le Roi en Jaune* ("The Red Book") from a hidden occult bookshop, per a receipt dated 22 Feb.
- Filed a police report on **30 August 1994** — nearly a year before her disappearance — describing being mugged near the Macallister's roof door by a large, older man in a silver robe and mask, who demanded to know "where the tunnels are."
- Within the [Night Floors](/locations/the-night-floors/), Castaigne and Mark Roark both independently confirmed a woman matching her description lives "up on 6" with "that creep of a salesman" — almost certainly Roark himself, a door-to-door salesman missing since 1933.
- A photograph of the Macallister Building from **1926** — nearly 70 years before her disappearance — includes Abigail Wright, apparently unaged.
- Her father, [Thomas Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/thomas-wright/), is a Nassau County police officer.
- The recovered book *A World Without Doors* concerns a girl named Abby who reaches a strange, timeless realm by drowning herself, battles a controlling force called the Phantom, and eventually becomes the Phantom herself to keep the realm from collapsing — a suspicious thematic echo of Abigail's own name and situation.

## Open Threads

- The 1926 photograph implies Abigail Wright — or someone identical to her — has existed in or around the Macallister for at least 70 years.
- Whether "Abigail" the missing artist and "Abby" the character in *A World Without Doors* are connected, and how, is unresolved.
- Never definitively located, dead or alive, before the Macallister Building was destroyed in Session 9.
