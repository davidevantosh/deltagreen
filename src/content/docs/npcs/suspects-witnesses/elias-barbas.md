## Role

Director of Forensic Science for the Massachusetts State Police, using the cover identity "Agent Exeter" to brief M-Cell on a fabricated version of Operation INDIA MOON. Secretly the summoner or vessel of the demon **Marbas**, and connected to the fates of Michael Witwer ("Agent Vega"), the King in Yellow mythos, and — per an old postcard — the Macallister Building's history.

## Session Notes

- **Session 15:** Briefed M-Cell as "Agent Exeter." Mortimer traced his real identity through his license plate the same night.
- **Session 16:** Personnel file shows 20+ years of distinguished service ending in a breakdown and administrative leave from June 2015.
- **Session 17:** Ambushed by the team at his house; fought them off with body armor and a mechanical guardian, "the Lion," which nearly killed Mortimer. Survived the first raid.
- **Session 18:** Killed by a sniper shot from Mortimer during the team's second raid. His house was searched and then burned, uncovering the Scribe, the cheat sheets on all of M-Cell, a Marbas ritual seal, and an impossible library behind his closet.

## Personal Effects Recovered

- A copper key to "room 616."
- A security card for Dorchester House.
- A pill case of rotten baby teeth.
- Hand-copied pages of *The King in Yellow*.
