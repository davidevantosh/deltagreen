## Role

Delta Green field agent, active 1958–1969, later an occasional "Friendly." Died of a heart attack in his East New York apartment four days before Session 10 begins.

## Known History

- Kept a Vietnam-era footlocker hidden in his Catskills cabin containing a sealed, unopened Delta Green envelope, a bloody suit, tear gas grenades, occult items, and files on a 1969 operation, "Manchester Shadow Team," whose four agents all died within two weeks of each other.
- Imprisoned his own transformed wife, [That Which Was Marlene](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/that-which-was-marlene/), in the cabin's septic tank rather than reporting or destroying her.
- Every family photo in his apartment had his late wife's eyes scratched out.

## Open Threads

- The unopened Delta Green envelope and the Manchester Shadow Team files were never followed up on.
