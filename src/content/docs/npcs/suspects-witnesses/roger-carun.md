## Role

46-year-old author of the Nightsea series, tenant of Apartment 3A in the [Macallister Building](/locations/operation-alice/macallister-building/).

## Session Notes

- **Session 3:** Let Murphy and Mason in for questions; his apartment was a mess. Mentioned the building is operated by a nonprofit called **ARTLIFE**.
- Separately, his publisher's editor, Carmen Wagner, reported him "gone AWOL" and considered filing a missing person report so his series' rights could revert to the publisher — leading to his editor's office pitching Agent Mason to ghost-write a continuation.
- A copy of "the play" (an early draft of *Le Roi en Jaune*) was found rewritten in his own apartment's computer, from his own point of view.
- **Session 8:** Encountered again, alive and drinking, in the [Night Floors'](/locations/operation-alice/the-night-floors/) smoking lounge alongside Mark Roark. Insisted Abigail Wright had "moved out, moved on" and was "not here, man."

## Open Threads

- Whether the Carun in his own apartment and the Carun seen in the Night Floors smoking lounge are the same person, a duplicate, or something else entirely is unresolved.
- Likely destroyed along with the Macallister Building in Session 9, though this was never directly confirmed.
