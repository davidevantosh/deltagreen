## Role

An old man in thick glasses, night manager of the [Night Floors](/locations/operation-alice/the-night-floors/), working for an unnamed "superintendent" who also lives upstairs there.

## Session Notes

- **Session 4:** His apartment was stacked wall-to-wall with old newspapers and magazines, none more recent than 1940, describing an alternate history (a Russo-Germanic conflict that never happened in reality). Confirmed Abigail Wright lives "up on 6" with "the salesman," paying rent quietly and on time, in cash. Agreed to hand over a basement key. Flew into a "murderous frenzy" when he caught Marlowe searching his room and looking at a handwritten genealogical tome, *The Imperial Dynasty of America* — was restrained by Mortimer.
- **Session 7:** A signature reading "A. Chastaigne," "G. Castaigne," and "H. Castaigne" was found inside an anatomical text in Michelle Vanfitz's library — suggesting the Castaigne name (and possibly this same individual, in some form) spans multiple generations.
