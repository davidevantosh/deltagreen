## Role

Architect credited with the 1953 conversion of the [Macallister Building](/locations/operation-alice/macallister-building/) from a private residence into an apartment building.

## Known History

- Early career (1905–1921) in Chicago, where he was called "the Picasso of architecture" for strange, misshapen "Thought Buildings."
- After what's variously described as a "religious revelation" or a "drug experience," moved to New York in 1921 and took up residence at an unnamed midtown hotel — later identified as the **Hotel Broadalbin**, near Madison Square Park.
- Designed nine buildings still standing in New York City, all comparatively mundane, each with small, obsessive flourishes.
- Suspected, following his 1950 disappearance, of drowning at least five children beginning in 1947 — possibly many more, per surviving research. Left behind the drowned body of five-year-old Isabelle Sauer, huge debts, and a log book tracking child deaths back to 1921.
- Vanished without a trace in 1950. Declared legally dead by his mother on **2 September 1960**.
- A 1926 photograph of the Macallister Building — taken before his 1953 renovation — includes [Abigail Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/), apparently unaged decades later.
- Appeared in Agent Mason's dream in Session 8, standing among the mourners at his late brother's funeral — Mason's brother having drowned in the Hudson River in an unsolved case.
