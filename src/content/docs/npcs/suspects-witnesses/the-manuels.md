## Role

Parents of [Thomas Manuel](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/thomas-manuel/). Desperate for news of their son, who they haven't heard from since March.

## Session Notes

- **Session 3:** Arthur left Mason a phone message; Elaine answered when Mason called back, very upset and desperate for information. Mason lied and told her the case was unrelated to Thomas. Letters found in Thomas's mail revealed the Manuels had tried to contact him directly at the building on **31 March**, after dark, and were turned away by a mysterious "man with a suitcase and a large grey dog," who told them Thomas no longer lived there.

## Open Threads

- They have not been told the truth about their son's disappearance or the fate of the building.
