## Role

Feminist author, tenant of Apartment 6B in the [Macallister Building](/locations/operation-alice/macallister-building/).

## Session Notes

- **Session 3:** Hostile; refused to let Murphy and Mortimer into her apartment.
- **Session 6:** Her apartment door was found ajar, revealing an impossible library-lounge space beyond a normal-looking front room. Confronted Mortimer over a red book (*Le Roi en Jaune*) he'd taken, claiming "Abby" gave it to her. Attacked him with mace and a tomahawk, cutting his arm; was shot in the arm and subdued in the struggle. Mabel dressed her wound.
- **Session 7:** Marched down to the Macallister's basement, along with Louis Post, and locked in per Delta Green's instructions. Did not resist.

## Open Threads

- Per Agent Marcus, she and Post were to be "taken care of overnight" by Delta Green cleanup — her ultimate fate was not shown directly. Likely died in the Session 9 demolition if not extracted beforehand.
