## Role

Comic book illustrator, tenant of Apartment 4A in the [Macallister Building](/locations/macallister-building/).

## Session Notes

- **Session 3:** Awkward but affable; let Mabel and Marlowe into his cluttered apartment. Marlowe found a huge baroque mirror hidden under his bed.
- **Session 7:** Ambushed Murphy and Marlowe with a carving knife when they broke into his apartment to check on him — Marlowe was badly cut before the team subdued him with gunfire and a beating, leaving him unconscious and bleeding out. His drawings, examined afterward, were nightmarish: drowned infants and disturbing imagery numbering hundreds of pages. Marched to the basement alongside Vanfitz and locked in per Delta Green's instructions.

## Open Threads

- Fate after being left in the basement was never shown directly. Likely died in the Session 9 demolition if not extracted beforehand.
