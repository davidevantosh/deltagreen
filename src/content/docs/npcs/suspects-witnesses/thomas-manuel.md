## Role

26-year-old painter. Lived in the apartment directly across the hall from [Abigail Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/) — the hidden microphone found in Session 1 was wired to his apartment.

## Session Notes

- **Session 2:** Interrogated by Mortimer, who confiscated his cassette tapes and viewed his disturbing basement paintings. Ordered to vacate and stay with his parents.
- **Session 3:** Police tape was found removed from his apartment door before the team arrived. His mail revealed the same March-onward pattern of late bills as Wright's, plus increasingly desperate letters from his parents, [Elaine and Arthur Manuel](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/the-manuels/) — including an account of the Manuels attempting to visit Thomas directly on **31 March**, after dark, and being turned away at the building by "a man with a suitcase and a large grey dog," who told them Thomas didn't live there anymore. Later that day, Mortimer was confronted by Manuel himself in his own apartment; the two had a tense but non-violent exchange, and Mortimer returned his keys — now fitted with a hidden audio bug. That night, audio from the bugged keys captured Manuel leaving his apartment, climbing repeated flights of stairs, and passing through a large door with a metal security bar — then the feed cut out entirely.

## Open Threads

- Manuel's ultimate fate was never confirmed. The audio trail strongly suggests he ascended into the [Night Floors](/locations/the-night-floors/) that same night, but he was never encountered there directly, and no further contact was made with him before the Macallister Building was destroyed.
