## Role

A cable repairman who entered the [Macallister Building](/locations/macallister-building/) on **20 March 1995** to disconnect a junction at a top-floor cable box, and became trapped in the [Night Floors](/locations/the-night-floors/) — subjectively experiencing what felt like years, though only months had passed outside.

## Session Notes

- **Session 7:** Emerged from hiding when he overheard the agents; emaciated, bearded, clearly traumatized, and begged to be freed.
- **Session 8:** Warned the agents never to sleep inside the Night Floors unless absolutely forced to — advice they heeded throughout. Confirmed he has a wife and child waiting for him. Escaped the Night Floors alongside the team, overwhelmed with relief at the outside world. Stayed the night at Mortimer's home in White Plains. The next morning, two Delta Green field agents (Carmen and Charles) arrived to collect him, telling him they'd "get him the help he needs."
