## Role

A man in his thirties found tied to a medical table in a hallway of the [Night Floors](/locations/the-night-floors/), mumbling to himself. Begged the agents to free him upon noticing them, identifying himself as "Agent Vega." When asked the year, he said 2015. When Murphy asked about a Michael Witwer, the man screamed that HE was Michael Witwer, a DEA agent.

## Session Notes

- **Session 4:** Before the agents could free him, the floor opened and the table began retracting into it. All available agents grabbed on, but couldn't stop it — the table and the man were pulled down into darkness. Looking into the hole afterward, the team saw a distant, pale face with empty eye sockets looking back up at them.

## Open Threads

- Whether he is truly a DEA agent named Michael Witwer, whether "2015" has any real meaning, and what pulled him down are all unresolved. His name was passed on to Agent Marcus, who noted it without further comment.
