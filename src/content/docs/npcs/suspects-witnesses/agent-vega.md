## Role

A man in his thirties found tied to a medical table in a hallway of the [Night Floors](/locations/operation-alice/the-night-floors/), mumbling to himself. Begged the agents to free him upon noticing them, identifying himself as "Agent Vega." When asked the year, he said **2015.** When Murphy asked about a Michael Witwer, the man screamed that HE was Michael Witwer, a DEA agent.

## Session Notes

- **Session 4 (1995):** Before the agents could free him, the floor opened and the table began retracting into it. All available agents grabbed on, but couldn't stop it — the table and the man were pulled down into darkness. Looking into the hole afterward, the team saw a distant, pale face with empty eye sockets looking back up at them.
- **Session 17 (2015):** Twenty years later, and in the very year he named, Michael Witwer's name resurfaces on a burner phone recovered from Elias Barbas's kitchen — messages from **Ophelia Sitri** searching for him, and a voicemail from a DEA agent. See [Agent Vega Revisited](/mysteries/agent-vega-revisited/).

## Open Threads
