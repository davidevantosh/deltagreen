Youth leader in Kips Bay, mentioned in a news article related to the case. Runs a local soup kitchen.

## Session 1 Notes

- Approached by [Agent Mabel](/characters/mabel/) and [Agent Mason](/characters/mason/) after a night of door-knocking.
- Met them at the soup kitchen Friday morning.
- Claimed no specific knowledge of the case, but revealed the [Macallister Building](/locations/macallister-building/) is a non-profit offering subsidised rent to artists, and that the resident artists are a tight-knit group who might know more.
