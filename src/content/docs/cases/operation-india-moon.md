## Status

**Closed.** M-Cell's target, Dr. Elias Barbas — secretly the summoner/vessel behind Marbas, and connected to Michael Witwer's ("Agent Vega's") disappearance and the Sitri cult — is dead, his mechanical guardian ("the Lion") destroyed, and his house burned. In the process, the entire team's real names, Bonds, and secrets were exposed to one another via Barbas's own surveillance files.

## Briefing

All five members of M-Cell were summoned, individually, to a birthday party invitation bearing a Purson seal — a Delta Green briefing in disguise. **Agent Exeter** (real identity: Dr. Elias Barbas, Director of Forensic Science for the Massachusetts State Police) briefed them on Operation INDIA MOON: five former Delta Green operatives had vanished simultaneously from the Dorchester House psychiatric facility in Boston, all previously exposed to an unnatural book.

## Objectives

1. Locate the missing former operatives. ⚠️ Never resolved — investigation was paused in favor of a new lead.
2. Investigate Agent Exeter after his true identity and erratic behavior raised suspicion. ✅ Confirmed as Dr. Elias Barbas, acting under the influence of the demon Marbas.
3. Neutralize the threat Barbas posed. ✅ Barbas killed, his house and its contents burned.
