## Status

**Closed.** Not a formally named Delta Green "Operation" — a single, off-books cleanup tasking with no handler, no debrief, and no official record beyond what the agents reported back. Concluded in one session with the immolation of a supernatural entity and the recovery of a Delta Green field agent's hidden effects.

## Briefing

Delta Green field agent **Clyde Baughman** — active 1958–1969, later an occasional "Friendly" — died of a heart attack in his East New York apartment. [Agent Mason](/characters/mason/) and [Agent Murphy](/characters/murphy/) were tasked by handler **Agent Hunter** with sweeping the apartment clean of any evidence of Delta Green's involvement before Baughman's adult children arrived, within 48 hours. Standard STATIC-adjacent protocol: no friendlies, no outside help.

## Objectives

1. Search and clear Baughman's apartment of DG-related material. ✅ Completed.
2. Investigate any leads found during the sweep. ✅ Led to Baughman's Catskills cabin.
3. Contain whatever was found there. ✅ A transformed entity — once Baughman's late wife, Marlene — was destroyed by fire in a septic tank.
