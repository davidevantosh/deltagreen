## Status

**Closed**, at extraordinarily high cost. The immediate threat (a Mi-Go biological operation embedded in rural Tennessee) was destroyed, but a rival faction extracted the remaining evidence before Delta Green could, and [Agent Murphy](/characters/murphy/) was taken into unexplained Delta Green custody for the better part of a year afterward.

## Briefing

FBI Special Agent in Charge James Derringer, a Delta Green friendly, requested help investigating Billy Ray Spivey — a young man in Groversville, Tennessee whose muscle tissue had been surgically replaced with an alien "protomatter" that granted him extreme, uncontrollable strength. M-Cell (Mortimer, Murphy, and Mason, later joined by Mabel) were sent undercover as FBI agents to find the source.

## Objectives

1. Determine the cause of Spivey's condition and any wider threat. ✅ Confirmed: a Mi-Go breeding/experimentation site.
2. Locate the source facility. ✅ An abandoned farm outside Groversville.
3. Contain or destroy the threat. ✅ The barn and its contents were destroyed by C4 and small-arms fire.
4. Protect exposed civilians. ⚠️ Partial — Jane Allen was extracted, but mass civilian casualties in Groversville were ultimately covered up as a hantavirus outbreak.
