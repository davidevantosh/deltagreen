## What is Delta Green?

Delta Green is a covert, unofficial wing of the U.S. federal government tasked with investigating and containing threats of an unnatural — often Lovecraftian — origin. Agents are pulled from existing careers (FBI, police, academia, medicine, military) and activated on a case-by-case basis, usually under a cover identity, with no official acknowledgment that Delta Green exists.

## The Campaign

New York City, 1995. Five agents with no prior connection to one another have been activated to form **M-Cell**, reporting to handler **Agent Marcus**.

- **Setting:** New York City, August 1995
