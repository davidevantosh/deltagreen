## What is Delta Green?

Delta Green is a covert, unofficial wing of the U.S. federal government tasked with investigating and containing threats of an unnatural — often Lovecraftian — origin. Agents are pulled from existing careers (FBI, police, academia, medicine, military) and activated on a case-by-case basis, usually under a cover identity, with no official acknowledgment that Delta Green exists.

## The Campaign

New York City, 1995 to Boston, 2015. Five agents with no prior connection to one another were activated to form **M-Cell** for Operation ALICE — and have been called back into service, on and off, ever since. Twenty years and three operations later, their real identities are no longer a secret from each other.

- **Setting:** New York City (1995) → Groversville, TN (2000) → Boston, MA (2015)
