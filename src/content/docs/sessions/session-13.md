## Summary

Dr. Bennett's protomatter-detection compound reveals the scale of what's happened to Groversville's water supply and its people. A night raid on an abandoned farm uncovers the source — and a rival organization moves in before Delta Green can finish the job.

## Timeline

1. Dr. Bennett arrives with a compound that reveals protomatter contamination — Murphy's torso is covered in invisible surgical scarring, and Groversville's tap water itself tests positive.
2. Jane Allen is extracted to Dr. Bennett's care in Knoxville.
3. Cell tower triangulation of Murphy's missing hours points the team to an abandoned farm east of town. Two nights of stakeouts reveal strange lights converging there.
4. Before dawn, the team raids the farm. Mortimer scouts the barn alone and finds a full **Mi-Go biological operation**: organic-mechanical instruments, operating tables, a vat of raw protomatter, inert humanoid husks, and two active Mi-Go creatures performing surgery on a dead child.
5. Mortimer plants C4 and detonates the barn as a Mi-Go patrol closes in on Mabel and Murphy; a firefight follows, ending with one Mi-Go killed.
6. A black helicopter lands: five armed commandos and a mocking, gold-toothed leader who taunts the team about "Majestic-12" before extracting the surviving evidence and destroying the rest with thermite.
7. The protomatter in the agents' own bodies instantly goes inert — Murphy's surgical incisions reopen catastrophically, and he nearly dies before Mabel stabilizes him and Mortimer races him to a trauma center.
8. Mass casualties across Groversville are covered up as a hantavirus outbreak. Murphy is later taken from his hospital bed into unexplained Delta Green custody, not returning to New York — or to himself — for months.
