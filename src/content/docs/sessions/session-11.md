## Summary

Mason, Murphy, and Mortimer are summoned by Agent Marcus to Knoxville, Tennessee, to assist FBI friendly James Derringer with a case gone strange: a young man with superhuman strength and non-human muscle tissue, and a small town full of "UFO" rumors that turn out to have real substance.

## Timeline

1. M-Cell (Mortimer, Murphy, Mason) are summoned via anonymous email to LaGuardia Airport; Agent Marcus briefs them in the elevator on the way up before vanishing into the crowd.
2. In Knoxville, FBI Special Agent in Charge **James Derringer** and forensic scientist **Dr. Bennett** show them surveillance footage of Billy Ray Spivey killing a gas station clerk with his bare hands — his skull "torn in half."
3. Dr. Bennett reveals Spivey's muscle tissue has been surgically replaced with an unknown proto-matter that mimics human muscle but grants extreme strength at the cost of his skeleton's structural integrity.
4. The team travels to Groversville, Tennessee, interviews Spivey's mother Angel, and hears about local "cattle mutilation" and UFO sightings.
5. Farmer Robert Gum's cow is missing an udder with no scarring; other farmers report similar mutilations.
6. The team visits Jane Allen's family — her mother Nancy is evasive about her daughter's whereabouts.
7. At RR Diner, Murphy vanishes mid-conversation while taking a phone call from his ex-wife. He doesn't return.
8. The next morning, Murphy reappears in the motel bathroom with no memory of the missing 12 hours — badly dehydrated and thirsty.
9. Searching a Delta Green storage unit (the "Green Box") at the local Handi-Mart, the team finds an extensive weapons and equipment cache, along with a warning note: "PLEASE exercise caution. We don't want a Waco."
10. Murphy provides a urine sample that comes back a swirling, fluorescent, oil-slick color.
