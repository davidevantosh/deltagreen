## Summary

With Vanfitz subdued, the team checks on her neighbor Louis Post — and nearly loses Marlowe to a knife. Deep in a second Night Floors excursion, they find a wall of photographs stretching back sixty years, and finally meet someone else trapped there.

## Timeline

1. Mortimer calls handler Agent Marcus to request cleanup for Vanfitz; told to finish the night's work first.
2. Mortimer, Murphy, and Marlowe check on Louis Post next door — he ambushes them with a knife, badly wounding Marlowe before being subdued by gunfire and a beating.
3. Murphy examines [Post's disturbing drawings](/evidence/operation-alice/posts-drawings/) and resists an intrusive mirror.
4. Post and Vanfitz are marched to the basement and locked away per Delta Green's instructions.
5. The rest of the team finds [more books](/evidence/operation-alice/vanfitz-library-books/) in Vanfitz's library, including *Maude Goes to the Masked Ball* — its illustrations feature Thomas Manuel's locket and a masked prince whose unmasked face is torn from the book.
6. The team enters the Night Floors a second time through a door in Vanfitz's library.
7. They find a wall of dozens of 1930s photographs of blank-faced people holding bottles — the last one captioned with Marlowe's real name, Miya Fukazawa. See [Miya Fukazawa's Photograph](/mysteries/miya-fukazawas-photograph/).
8. They recover the mastiff Abe in the ballroom of dancing marionettes, glimpse figures across a bottomless chasm, and receive [a masquerade invitation](/evidence/operation-alice/the-invitation/) from a clockwork figure.
9. Mabel finds [a Yellow Sign newspaper scrap](/evidence/operation-alice/yellow-sign-newspaper/) — showing it triggers an involuntary, shared compulsion across the whole team.
10. Mortimer discovers the hallway is a stage set in front of an audience of marionettes; the team flees as the marionette audience disperses into the halls.
11. Marlowe has a full breakdown; Mason comforts her, and the two exchange real names, forming a Delta Green Bond.
12. A man steps out of hiding, begging for help: [David Langford](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/david-langford/), trapped since March.
