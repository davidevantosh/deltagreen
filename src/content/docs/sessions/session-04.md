## Summary

A live transmission on the recovered radio and a knock at the window bring the team back together. They head upstairs, and the roof access door opens onto the [Night Floors](/locations/operation-alice/the-night-floors/) — a vast, shifting pocket dimension with its own residents, history, and rules.

## Timeline

1. The walkie-talkie crackles to life on its own; Mortimer answers as "Alan." The call cuts out, followed by tapping at the window — Mabel, checking in after his poker game.
2. The team leaves an update for handler Agent Marcus at a payphone, asking whether to proceed upstairs. No callback comes.
3. The team heads upstairs, weapons drawn. Notices the red ball is gone and a black hair has been carefully placed across Roger Carun's door jamb.
4. Marlowe and Mabel search Carun's apartment, finding [his hidden files](/evidence/operation-alice/carun-hidden-files/) — a rewritten play script and a set of cryptic short stories.
5. Mortimer, Murphy, and Mason ascend to the empty third floor and open the roof access door — it opens onto the **Night Floors** instead.
6. The team meets Mark Roark in the smoking lounge, who tells them Abigail is "up on 6" with "that creep of a salesman" and directs them to Castaigne, the night manager.
7. Castaigne confirms Abigail lives on 6. While Mason distracts him for a basement key, Marlowe searches his room and finds a strange genealogical tome — Castaigne catches her and flies into a rage; Mortimer restrains him.
8. The hallways rearrange behind the team. They find a recursive, infinite room through a locked door, and a hallway where two people are shot by three gas-masked men — the "bodies" turn out to be marionettes carrying [Carter and Burbach's effects](/evidence/operation-alice/carters-and-burbachs-effects/).
9. Down another hallway, the team finds a man strapped to a medical table calling himself "Agent Vega" / Michael Witwer, claiming the year is 2015. The floor opens and swallows him before he can be freed.
