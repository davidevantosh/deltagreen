## Summary

M-Cell finally tracks down the hidden bookshop where Abigail Wright bought *Le Roi en Jaune*, uncovering a century-old ritual and a cache of period military gear along the way. With no innocents left inside, Delta Green authorizes the incursion's containment — and the Macallister Building burns.

## Timeline

1. Murphy arranges to meet Thomas Wright near the bookshop; Mabel is a no-show, still lost in his fugue state.
2. The team finds [the Bookshop](/locations/operation-alice/the-bookshop/), hidden behind a rusted security gate. Its proprietor, Bob, confirms Abigail bought the red book there.
3. Mason finds the Hygromanteia and a hidden [cache of gas masks and shotguns](/evidence/operation-alice/gas-mask-cache/) among the stacks. Marlowe meets Moseby, who claims to have written *Le Roi en Jaune* and warns the Macallister is "a bad path."
4. While Bob fetches Mortimer a second copy of the red book, Marlowe steals [his ledgers](/evidence/operation-alice/bobs-ledgers/) from a strongbox.
5. Meanwhile, Des (Mabel) loses all of Tuesday to a fever dream of the Night Floors, waking Wednesday to a disturbing tape message from his wife Teri.
6. Mabel reads two recovered occult texts and reunites with the team at Madison Square Park — a search for the Hotel Broadalbin turns up nothing.
7. Mabel reads the stolen ledgers: nothing but "Jabberwocky" translated into Farsi, repeated for a thousand pages, dated back to 1895. Mason reads *Le Roi en Jaune* itself directly — "his mind is opened."
8. The team calls handler Agent Marcus, who confirms the Macallister Building is top priority for urgent containment, with no innocents remaining inside.
9. At 3:00 AM, the team returns to the building, sets the gas appliances running, and times a spark. They leave before it explodes, destroying the building entirely.
10. In a final call with Agent Marcus, the team is stood down to rest and lie low — Delta Green will debrief, and any remaining leads can be picked up in the future.
11. Mortimer spots the masked figure one last time near the alley outside; the team gives chase and finds only an old gramophone, which starts playing on its own.
12. **Epilogue, weeks later:** Murphy finally opens his safety deposit box at Chase Manhattan — inside is [a 19th-century jeweled crown](/evidence/operation-alice/the-crown/).

**Operation ALICE is closed** as of this session — the incursion has been contained via the destruction of the Macallister Building, and Delta Green has stood the team down. Several threads remain explicitly open for the future per Agent Marcus's own words.
