## Summary

M-Cell raids Elias Barbas's house and finds a nightmare made real: a mechanical guardian identical to the "impossible" napkin sketches found in Abigail Wright's apartment twenty years earlier. The raid nearly kills Dick Byrne.

## Timeline

1. The team breaches Barbas's house — Dick and Miya from the back, Calvin and Dutch from the front — after spotting Barbas inside wearing ceremonial robes and hauling boxes of identical red leather-bound books.
2. Inside: the electricity is off, the smell of oil is overwhelming, dishes lie smashed but clean, and the living room is filled with disassembled industrial machinery and a strange standing contraption by the stairs.
3. The contraption activates: **The Lion**, a mechanical guardian matching the "impossible mechanical diagrams" found in Session 2 of Operation ALICE, twenty years prior. It nearly decapitates Calvin, tearing open his arm instead.
4. In the ensuing fight, Barbas (wearing body armor under his pajamas) shoots at the team; Miya sets the house on fire with gasoline.
5. The Lion corners Dick in the kitchen and crushes him against the wall, breaking ribs and rupturing organs — critically wounding him.
6. Miya carries Dick's dying body out, and the team flees in the buick, the Lion in pursuit but unable to catch them. They deliver Dick to CHA Everett Hospital and vanish before he's stabilized.
7. Delta Green orders the team to finish the job: return, terminate Barbas, and search the house.
8. Miya recovers a burner phone from Barbas's kitchen, marked with a demonic seal for **Sitri** — containing messages from Ophelia Sitri searching for a missing Michael Witwer, and a voicemail from a DEA agent.
