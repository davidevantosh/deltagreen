## Summary

M-Cell spends the day digging through records — military files, library archives, and old newspapers — and comes away with a much fuller (and much stranger) picture of the Macallister Building's history. That night's return to the Night Floors ends in a violent confrontation with one of the building's tenants.

## Timeline

1. Mortimer researches [Carter and Burbach](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/carter-and-burbach/) at the marshals office, uncovering their 1955 murder file and a person of interest: Virgil Griffith, of White Plains.
2. Murphy digs through FBI records with his colleague Sandra — finds that Macallister tenants have made no calls or spent money since March, and recovers [Wright's 1994 police report](/evidence/wrights-1994-police-report/) describing a mugging near the building's roof door by a man demanding "where the tunnels are."
3. Mark Roark is confirmed as a real missing person from 1933 — a Fuller Brush salesman, last known address the Hotel Broadalbin.
4. Marlowe and Mabel research architect [Asa Daribondi](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/asa-daribondi/) at the library, uncovering his suspected history of child murders, his connection to the Hotel Broadalbin, and a 1926 photograph of the Macallister Building featuring Abigail Wright.
5. Mortimer and Murphy pull Carter and Burbach's full military records.
6. That night, Marlowe is accosted at home by a masked figure leaving [$10,000 in cash](/evidence/the-cash-payment/) on her doorstep; she's injured trying to give chase.
7. The team asks handler Agent Marcus about "[Operation BRISTOL](/mysteries/operation-bristol/)" — Delta Green has no record of it.
8. M-Cell returns to the Macallister for a second Night Floors excursion, first investigating Michelle Vanfitz's apartment, whose interior opens onto an impossible library.
9. Mortimer takes [*Le Roi en Jaune*](/evidence/le-roi-en-jaune/) from Vanfitz's bedside; she attacks with mace and a tomahawk, wounding him, before being shot and subdued.
