## Summary

M-Cell spends a full day cataloguing [Abigail Wright's apartment](/locations/operation-alice/macallister-building/), uncovering several occult-flavored items of interest. [Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/) breaks off to interrogate the neighbor across the hall, [Thomas Manuel](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/thomas-manuel/), whose basement studio contains a painting that shouldn't exist — a depiction of the exact clown scene from Session 1. The team also learns each other's real names for the first time this session, and the evening ends with a string of unsettling personal moments for nearly every agent.

## Timeline

1. **[Cataloguing the Apartment](/moments/operation-alice/cataloguing-the-apartment/)** — Marlowe, Mason, Mabel, and Murphy bag and tag items of interest: a [Farsi receipt](/evidence/operation-alice/farsi-receipt/), [Hotel Broadalbin letterhead](/evidence/operation-alice/hotel-broadalbin-letterhead/), a [play transcript page](/evidence/operation-alice/play-transcript-page/), a [butcher paper map](/evidence/operation-alice/butcher-paper-map/), and the [mechanical diagrams](/evidence/operation-alice/mechanical-diagrams/) "The Lion" and "The Scribe."
2. **[Voices Through the Wall](/moments/operation-alice/voices-through-the-wall/)** — Marlowe hears laughing or crying; investigates with Murphy, finds nobody.
3. **[Interrogating Thomas Manuel](/moments/operation-alice/interrogating-manuel/)** — Mortimer crosses the hall, confiscates cassette tapes, and discovers [Manuel's Paintings](/mysteries/manuels-paintings/) — including one matching [The Clown](/mysteries/the-clown/) from Session 1. Manuel is ordered to leave.
4. The team breaks for orange chicken at 2:08 PM.
5. **[Hauling Manuel Out](/moments/operation-alice/hauling-manuel-out/)** — Mortimer and Mason return when Manuel dithers about leaving.
6. **[Sarah's Urgent Call](/moments/operation-alice/sarahs-urgent-call/)** — Mortimer's wife, DEA agent Sarah Clarke, pages him for an urgent meeting the next day.
7. **[Searching Manuel's Apartment](/moments/operation-alice/searching-manuels-apartment/)** — Mortimer and Mason find it austere and hotel-like, with impossibly fresh food dated to late March.
8. Marlowe reviews [Abigail's Mail](/evidence/operation-alice/abigails-mail/) — utility termination notices from March, yet services still run.
9. The team calls it a day at 5:25 PM, agreeing to reconvene 9:00 AM Saturday.
10. **[The Bottle Incident](/moments/operation-alice/the-bottle-incident/)** — Marlowe, helping Sal Mazzi move boxes, is accosted by an unidentified man demanding "his bottle."
11. **[Dragon on the Doorstep](/moments/operation-alice/dragon-on-the-doorstep/)** — Mortimer finds a paper dragon waiting for him at home in White Plains.
12. **[Dinner with Gloria](/moments/operation-alice/dinner-with-gloria/)** — Mason's partner says something unsettling he isn't sure he actually heard.
