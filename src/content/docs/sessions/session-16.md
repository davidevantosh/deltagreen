## Summary

M-Cell tours the Dorchester House psychiatric facility and finds two ghosts from Operation ALICE living there as patients — along with a night-shift mystery of their own, and a discovery that turns their attention away from the missing operatives entirely.

## Timeline

1. Calvin wakes to find a live goldfish in his hotel sink — an echo of the goldfish sculpture from the Night Floors, twenty years on.
2. The team meets Dr. Dallan at Dorchester House, receiving files on the five missing patients: Thomas Hou, Cal Diassa, Lila Mac, Kim Plaslad, and Rae Bilabass — all former operatives, all suffering psychological aftereffects consistent with unnatural exposure.
3. On the tour, Calvin recognizes two patients from twenty years ago: **Morgan Goldstein** (his own "disappeared" former literary agent) and **David Langford**, both of whom pretend not to know him.
4. On the third floor ("the Pokey"), the team meets **Ed Wist** and **Tim Bael** — also recognized from the Night Floors. Wist claims the missing patients vanished "like a camera trick," restraints and locked doors undisturbed. Bael says they're gone because "they don't exist yet" but will return.
5. Reviewing security footage, the team finds the actual disappearance recordings corrupted beyond recovery — a suspiciously convenient failure.
6. Investigating after hours, Dick tails orderly Richard Brice to a gym locker — inside is the long-mummified body of an unidentified child.
7. Chief Deputy Bass Reeves sends Dick a personnel file on Barbas: 20+ years of distinguished service, ending in a sudden breakdown and administrative leave beginning June 2015.
8. The team calls in on the sat-phone; Delta Green orders them to pause the missing-patients investigation entirely — Exeter/Barbas is now the priority target.
9. That night, the team stakes out Barbas's Medford house and finds an old, undelivered postcard on the porch, addressed cryptically to someone named "M" and mentioning "Abigail" and a man named "Moseby."
