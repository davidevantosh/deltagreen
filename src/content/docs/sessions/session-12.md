## Summary

Mortimer arrives to reinforce the team as they dig deeper into Groversville's missing-time epidemic — turning up a dead "UFO guy," an impossibly pregnant Jane Allen, and unmistakable evidence that whatever's happening here goes well beyond one boy's strength.

## Timeline

1. Mortimer arrives in Groversville and rejoins Mabel and the team.
2. Mabel and Mortimer investigate the reservoir and spot a black helicopter circling overhead before vanishing east.
3. At town hall, Bud Aldrich provides utility plans. Mortimer notices a tail — first a white van (traced to journalist Frank Carincola), then a black sedan whose driver flashes a laser sight and claims government affiliation before being run off.
4. The team interrogates Billy Ray's friends at the high school, then presses Bud Aldrich again about his own missing time — he denies it, unconvincingly.
5. At the motel, the team learns "Scott Adams" (the "UFO guy") has been missing since before their arrival — but his room shows signs of recent occupancy.
6. In Room 6, they find **Jane Allen**, hiding — three weeks pregnant but already at full term, having also experienced missing time.
7. In Room 5, Mortimer finds Scott Adams dead in a bloody bathtub — a white, non-human growth erupts from his head and destroys Mortimer's pistol before he kills it with his backup weapon.
8. Sheriff Dan Oakley is brought in to secure the scene. A laptop is recovered from Adams's room.
