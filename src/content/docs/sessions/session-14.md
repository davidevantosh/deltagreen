## Summary

An interstitial session, not a mission — a look at where each member of M-Cell ends up across the fifteen years between Operation CONVERGENCE and the events that follow. No case attached; this is pure character background, delivered directly rather than played out scene by scene.

## What Happened to Each Agent

**[Dick Byrne (Mortimer)](/characters/mortimer/)** — Appointed U.S. Marshal for the Southern District of New York in 2001, a post he holds for 14 years. Snubs his best friend Reggie for the Chief Deputy role, costing the friendship. Divorced, increasingly isolated, he buys a Catskills cabin as a retreat. Now 62, living alone in White Plains, with intermittent explosive disorder and a totemic attachment to his 9mm pistol.

**[Dutch Gaper (Murphy)](/characters/murphy/)** — Never hears from Delta Green again after his Groversville ordeal. Now 64, still on Jamaica Ave, with a new dog (Fozzy) after his ex-wife took Gonzo to Michigan. Coasts toward retirement at a desk job arranged by his now-estranged colleague Sandra. Writes weird fiction (*The Adventures of Drake Obsidian*) and sees a therapist, Dr. Carolyn Crowell. Has become semi-fluent in Tartessian — an extinct Bronze Age language — for reasons even he can't fully explain.

**[Miya Fukazawa (Marlowe)](/characters/marlowe/)** — Now 50, still in the same Chinatown apartment. Runs a small cybercrime syndicate with hacker "Acid" (4C1D) as her right hand. Quietly wealthy, deliberately unassuming. Hasn't worked with Delta Green since 1995.

**[Calvin Cooper (Mason)](/characters/mason/)** — Now 57, two kids, living in Maplewood, NJ. Moved from journalism into TV writing after his Catholic exposé was published by "real journalists." When his literary agent Morgan Goldstein was "disappeared" by Delta Green after publishing one too many Yellow Sign-inflected works, Calvin took the warning and reined in the occult influence in his own writing. Remains active with Delta Green, called on now for academic/occult expertise rather than fieldwork. Spends years obsessively researching the Castaigne family after finding their name in the Hygromanteia — see [The Castaigne Dossier](/mysteries/the-castaigne-dossier/).

**[Des Butler (Mabel)](/characters/mabel/)** — Remains at the Department of Energy, now with PTSD from Operation CONVERGENCE. In 2007, his best friend Barry Romano dies of an apparent heroin overdose shortly after describing a strange film shoot — Des chooses not to investigate. Divorces his wife Teri in 2010 after her own breakdown. Forms a bond with his therapist, Kathy Schmidt, and makes exactly one new friend.
