## Summary

The team, now guiding David Langford, spends over twelve hours searching for a way out of the Night Floors. What they find along the way — a working ritual circle, a near-glimpse of a masked figure's face, and a room full of drying blood — is only the beginning of the toll it takes on them.

## Timeline

1. David Langford confirms he's been trapped since 20 March, warns the team never to sleep in the Night Floors.
2. Mason leads the search for an exit, passing lost hospital patients and finding an active Purson summoning ritual mid-invocation.
3. Marlowe spots gold and silver cloth vanishing through a doorway; Murphy finds the room behind it empty.
4. Murphy spots an aged version of a Chase Manhattan bank officer disappearing into a room marked "PROJECTION" — through its window, glimpses a masked figure beginning to remove a white mask before the projector fails.
5. Mortimer tries navigating by scent with his eyes shut; a friendly, unintelligible waiter offers food, which the team declines.
6. Mason finds a golden goldfish sculpture. The team hears distant automatic gunfire and finds an empty, blood-tracked ballroom.
7. The team finally stumbles back into the smoking lounge, now occupied by Mark Roark and Roger Carun, who insist Abigail Wright has "moved on."
8. After 12+ hours inside, the team exits the Night Floors — and immediately suffers a collective breakdown: Mortimer empties his gun into a wall and flees; Murphy and Marlowe break down sobbing; Mason notices the goldfish sculpture has become living flesh.
9. The team eats at the Chinese takeout place, given free food by the owner. Mortimer debriefs Agent Marcus, who orders Post and Vanfitz left to Delta Green cleanup and Langford taken home overnight.
10. That night: Mason dreams of his brother's funeral and sees Asa Daribondi among the mourners; Mortimer dreams of his father's locked wartime diary; Mabel spirals into a catatonic fugue state, desperate to share the Yellow Sign but unable to move.
11. Mason shares the Yellow Sign by scrawling it into his manuscript and sending it to his agent.
12. Marlowe slaps a plate of cookies out of Peggy Funk's hands.
13. Two Delta Green field agents arrive at Mortimer's home to collect David Langford.

## New Characters Introduced

None new — but a Delta Green Bond formed between Mason and Marlowe at the end of Session 7 defines much of this session's emotional throughline.
