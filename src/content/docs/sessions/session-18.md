## Summary

M-Cell finishes what they started: the Lion is destroyed, Barbas is killed, and his house is burned to the ground. But not before the team finds his private files — cheat sheets on every one of them, real names and all, ending twenty years of code-name anonymity in a single afternoon.

## Timeline

1. While Dick recovers in the ICU, Calvin is unnerved by a reflection of Esther Samigina watching him through his own bathroom mirror — a window into somewhere else entirely.
2. Research reveals Samigina is also the name of a demon from the Ars Goetia — and Calvin recalls "Sami" from the Macallister Building basement, twenty years earlier.
3. A crate of Vietnam-War-era weapons and equipment arrives anonymously at Miya's hotel.
4. Dick discharges himself against medical advice. The team returns to Barbas's house — undamaged, as if the Wednesday raid never happened — and clears the street with a fake gas leak.
5. A second confrontation with the Lion ends in its destruction via claymore mines and shotgun fire.
6. Dick snipes Barbas dead through an upstairs window as he grabs a weapon.
7. Searching the house: a smashed mold, an Ars Goetia printout covered in two different handwritings, books with words clipped out, and — upstairs — the "Scribe," a strange automated writing/binding machine, alongside a box of identical red leather notebooks (**the same stock as the original Le Roi en Jaune**) and **cheat sheets on every member of M-Cell**, listing real names, addresses, and every Bond, including details concealed from Delta Green itself.
8. A meter-wide **Marbas** demonic seal is found etched beneath Barbas's futon. His body carries a copper key to "room 616," a security card for "the Dorchester House," a pill case of rotten baby teeth, and hand-copied pages of *The King in Yellow*.
9. Behind a barred door in the closet, Calvin glimpses an impossible library on the other side of what should be solid wall.
10. As sirens approach, the team burns the house with a white phosphorus grenade and escapes to Miya's hotel suite — where they finally read the cheat sheets together. Code names are over. M-Cell now know each other completely.

## New Characters Introduced

None new this session — but the reveal of everyone's real names, addresses, and Bonds to each other redefines every existing character relationship going forward.

## Case Status

**Operation INDIA MOON is closed** as of this session. The immediate threat (Barbas/Marbas) is destroyed, but the five originally missing Dorchester House patients were never found, and several major threads — the impossible library behind the closet door, the Boxer Hotel, Agent Vega/Witwer's fate — remain completely open.
