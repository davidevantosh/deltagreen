## Summary

Four years after Operation ALICE, Mason and Murphy are activated for a routine cleanup: sweep the apartment of a deceased Delta Green field agent before his family arrives. The routine part doesn't last — a lead in his paperwork sends them to a remote Catskills cabin, where they find a footlocker of Delta Green history and something much worse hidden in the property's septic tank.

## Timeline

1. Mason and Murphy each receive an anonymous encrypted email summoning them to the post office headquarters. Handler **Agent Hunter** briefs them: sweep the apartment of Clyde Baughman, a Delta Green field agent (active 1958–1969) who died four days ago, before his children arrive within 48 hours.
2. At Baughman's East New York apartment, the agents note an unusual military-grade lock key on his ring, and search the apartment — finding little beyond old paperwork and unsettling family photos, including his late wife Marlene with her eyes scratched out of every picture.
3. Six hours of paperwork turns up one lead: Baughman owned a cabin in the Catskills.
4. The next morning, Murphy and Mason drive up to the cabin, finding a Vietnam-era footlocker under the bed containing a sealed, unopened Delta Green envelope, a bloody suit, tear gas grenades, occult items, and files on a defunct 1969 operation — "Manchester Shadow Team" — whose four agents all died within two weeks of each other.
5. Investigating a suspicious dig site, the agents find a padlocked septic tank hatch — and a woman's voice pleading from inside.
6. What emerges is **[That Which Was Marlene](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/that-which-was-marlene/)** — Baughman's wife, transformed by "some awful demonic prayer" into something inhuman and enslaved, then imprisoned by her own husband. A brutal fight ends with her burned alive in the tank.
7. The agents report to Agent Hunter, who orders them to finish the job and remove the remains. They return that night, confirm the entity is destroyed, and recover what's left.
8. Back in New York, exhausted, Mason and Murphy exchange their real names for the first time: Calvin, and Dutch.
