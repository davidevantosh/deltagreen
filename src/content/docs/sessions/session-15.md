## Summary

Twenty years after Operation ALICE, all five members of M-Cell receive an ornate invitation to a stranger's birthday party — a Delta Green summons in disguise, leading them to Boston and a briefing from a handler whose own conduct raises immediate questions.

## Timeline

1. Each agent receives a FedEx package containing an invitation to a birthday party for "Richard Zielony" at Gateway Bridges Restaurant, Dorchester, Boston — embossed with a green triangle. Tearing it open reveals a hidden handwriting sample and a gold Purson seal; saying "Purson" aloud sets off a fire alarm at Dutch's office.
2. Dick recognizes the hidden text as a "request exemplar" — a handwriting-comparison sample, the kind used to identify a suspect.
3. The team converges on Boston separately across the weekend, each checking into different hotels.
4. Monday night, Des scopes out the restaurant in advance.
5. Tuesday night, Dick surveils the restaurant beforehand and notes a Massachusetts State Police vehicle arriving — driven by a man who turns out to be their handler.
6. At 8:00 PM, M-Cell reunites for the first time in 20 years and meets **Agent Exeter** — pill-popping, oddly scented, briefing them on **Operation INDIA MOON**: five former Delta Green operatives have vanished simultaneously from a Boston psychiatric facility, all previously exposed to an unnatural book.
7. That night, Dick has Exeter's license plate traced — it belongs to **Dr. Elias Barbas**, Director of Forensic Science for the Massachusetts State Police. Calvin separately researches **Dr. Dallan**, the facility's director, finding nothing unusual.
