## Summary

Five newly-activated Delta Green agents — [Mortimer](/characters/mortimer/), [Murphy](/characters/murphy/), [Mabel](/characters/mabel/), [Marlowe](/characters/marlowe/), and [Mason](/characters/mason/) — are briefed by handler [Agent Marcus](/npcs/delta-green/agent-marcus/) on **[Operation ALICE](/cases/operation-alice/)**, the disappearance of artist [Abigail Wright](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/abigail-wright/). Split into pairs, they investigate leads and stake out Wright's building overnight, culminating in an illegal night entry to her apartment and an official visit the next morning with NYPD.

## Timeline

1. **[The Valise Briefing](/moments/operation-alice/valise-briefing/)** — Washington Square Park, 4:45 PM Thursday.
2. **[The Busking Clown](/moments/operation-alice/busking-clown/)** — Mason unsettled by a street performer; Mortimer notices nothing.
3. The team reviews Marcus's valise at a nearby pizza restaurant. **[Mabel's cover is nearly blown](/moments/operation-alice/mabels-cover-blown/)** by poker buddy Abad.
4. The team splits:
   - **Murphy** takes the FBI/police reports home to Jamaica Ave for analysis.
   - **Mabel & Mason** head to the Macallister area to chase the Anthony Scott lead.
   - **Mortimer & Marlowe** stake out the [Macallister Building](/locations/operation-alice/macallister-building/).
5. Mabel and Mason door-knock the neighborhood, learn Anthony Scott will be at a soup kitchen the next morning, and go home.
6. **[Night Break-In at the Macallister](/moments/operation-alice/night-break-in/)** — Mortimer and Marlowe enter Wright's apartment at 23:00, find a hidden microphone, and are forced to flee after Marlowe's failed lockpick attempt makes noise.
7. Friday morning: Mabel and Mason meet [Anthony Scott](/npcs/suspects-witnesses/anthony-scott/) at the soup kitchen and learn about the building's tenant community.
8. **[Official Visit with Detective Giuradanda](/moments/operation-alice/official-apartment-visit/)** — All five agents regroup at 10:00 AM, tour the apartment officially, and recover the [FBI evidence box](/evidence/operation-alice/fbi-evidence-box/).
